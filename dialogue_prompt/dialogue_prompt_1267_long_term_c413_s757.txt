The student is sitting in a pull-out special education classroom and is expected to complete a difficult independent math worksheet.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your math worksheet. Please complete these problems independently.

STUDENT: (Leaves the pencil on the desk) Did you see the new movie trailer that dropped yesterday? The main character drives a red sports car.

TUTOR: We need to focus on the math worksheet right now.

STUDENT: Stop interrupting me! I'm talking about the movie. The car goes zero to sixty in three seconds.

TUTOR: Please just start with problem number one.

STUDENT: I'm not doing this stupid math! And tell him to stop looking at me or I'm going to smash his face!
