SCENARIO

The student is attempting to complete an academic task using separate materials while seated in a large, noisy regular classroom.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here are your separate materials for the assignment. The rest of the class is starting their group discussions now, so you can begin working on this.

STUDENT: Please don't make a big deal out of giving me separate materials in front of everyone else.

TUTOR: Alright, just try to focus on your work while the other groups talk.

STUDENT: There are too many things going on in here right now and the other students are disturbing me.

TUTOR: Keep trying to concentrate on the task in front of you.

STUDENT: I can't sort out all this noise. If it’s not calm, sensible and quiet, I just cannot think!
