Charlie is sitting at a table in his classroom during reading class and is asked to read a paragraph from a book aloud.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Charlie, it is time for our reading lesson. Please look at this book and read the first paragraph aloud.

STUDENT: (Pushes the book away and shakes his head to refuse to work)

TUTOR: We need to do our reading work now. Please start reading.

STUDENT: (Starts to melt down, whining and engaging in avoidance behaviors by turning his body away from the reading material)

TUTOR: Charlie, you need to stay at the table and finish this assignment.

STUDENT: (Screams loudly, pounds his fist on the table, and leaves his chair to escape the reading work)
