SCENARIO

The student is working independently on a practice reading passage about elephants and its corresponding questions.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for independent practice. Please read the passage about the elephant and complete the corresponding questions.

STUDENT: (Taps pencil rapidly on the desk) I'm already done with it.

TUTOR: That was very fast. Did you read the entire passage?

STUDENT: No, I just read paragraph one and two on the elephant. Then I saw the questions and then—um—it said paragraph six. So I just went to paragraph six.

TUTOR: You need to read every paragraph to prepare for the TAKS test. Please sit still and read it again.

STUDENT: (Starts stomping his feet, then stands up and begins walking around the room) I'm not doing that. I don't want to be a schoolboy kissing up to the teacher just to get a lot of good scores.
