DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Matthew, it is your turn to select the answer on the Interactive Whiteboard for our whole class activity.

STUDENT: (Approaches the front of the room but sits quietly, looking up at the Interactive Whiteboard.)

TUTOR: If you can't reach the board, you can use the computer keyboard on the desk right there.

STUDENT: (Places his hand over the computer keyboard but struggles to apply the precise touch and pressure required on each key.)

TUTOR: Just try to press the keys carefully, one at a time.

STUDENT: (His hand slips off the computer keyboard due to his motor difficulties, and he remains unable to access the Interactive Whiteboard because of its inaccessible height.)
