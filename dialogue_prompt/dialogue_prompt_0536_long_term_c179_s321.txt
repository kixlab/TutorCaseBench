SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a 10-minute teacher-led math lesson on number properties while seated at their desk.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at the whiteboard for our math lesson on number properties. Please sit quietly and look at the examples.

STUDENT: (Twists body away from the location of the learning stimuli on the white board for more than 5 seconds) I'm not looking at that. (Starts talking to the student at the next desk)

TUTOR: You need to focus on your own assignment.

STUDENT: (Continues speaking to others while the teacher talks) Make me! (Forcefully pushes the student next to him)

TUTOR: Stop that right now and get back to work.

STUDENT: (Stands up, leaving the desk so no body part touches the chair for more than 5 seconds) I'm done with this! (Shouts, raising voice beyond an average volume practiced in the class)
