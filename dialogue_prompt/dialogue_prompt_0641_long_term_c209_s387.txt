SCENARIO

Shen is sitting at his desk in the general classroom during instructional time to complete his assigned written work.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for instructional time. Please look at the assigned work on your desk and begin.

STUDENT: (Sits quietly at first and watches the other students, trying to follow their actions.)

TUTOR: Go ahead and read the instructions on the paper.

STUDENT: (Looks at the English words on the page, then becomes antsy and starts shifting around in his seat.)

TUTOR: You need to do the assignment. What do you think you should do next?

STUDENT: I don’t understand, so I won’t do it. (Gets up out of his seat and begins walking around the classroom without permission.)
