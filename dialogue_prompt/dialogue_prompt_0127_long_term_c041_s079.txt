SCENARIO

The class is standing in a circle for a group reading activity to figure out the meaning of a text extract about a battle at the gates of Troy.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Everyone, please stand in a circle. We are going to read this text extract about what might be happening in a battle at the gates of Troy.

STUDENT: (Slouches on a chair in the corner instead of joining the circle)

TUTOR: Let's figure out the meaning together. What do you think it means when the text says he was 'struck him on the ear'?

STUDENT: He hit him hard on the ear.

TUTOR: Okay. Now we are going to take turns reading one phrase at a time. Here is the book, it is your turn to read.

STUDENT: (Becomes angry and hostile when the book is given to him, unable to sound out the words)
