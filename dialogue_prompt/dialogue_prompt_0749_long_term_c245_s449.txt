SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is asked to answer a direct question and express her opinion in front of her classmates during a whole-class lesson.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Santa, please answer the question and express your opinion before the class.

STUDENT: (Sits at her desk and does nothing) I didn't understand something, but I used to do nothing as I didn't dare to ask for an explanation neither teachers nor classmates.

TUTOR: Just give it a try. Tell everyone what you think.

STUDENT: Why should I say something if nobody cares... I don't know, it was unpleasant to me.

TUTOR: We are waiting for your answer. Please speak up so the class can hear you.

STUDENT: (Refuses to answer the question and keeps quiet) It's better not to say anything and keep quiet so that nobody notices you rather than let them laugh at you.
