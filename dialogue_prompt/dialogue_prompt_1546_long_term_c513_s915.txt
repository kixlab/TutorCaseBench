DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (Keeps head down, not looking at the instructor or the media on the screen.) I don't see anything.

TUTOR: Just give it a try and follow along with the rest of the class.

STUDENT: (Gets out of the seat and speaks loudly, interrupting the instruction) I said I don't know! This is too hard and I'm not doing it!
