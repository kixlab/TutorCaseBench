DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Jimmy, we are starting the English writing assignment now. Please get your paper out and begin working.

STUDENT: (Keeps his head down on the desk without taking out any materials)

TUTOR: You need to complete the assignment and turn it in so you can get a grade.

STUDENT: I do the work, I just don't turn it in and sometimes I do it in a different way.

TUTOR: If you have the work done, you should just hand it in right now.

STUDENT: (Scoffs) (Puts his head back down to sleep to drown out the classroom environment, refusing to speak or engage any further while his peers are present)
