SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Gail is taking a Science test and turning in an extra credit project in her classroom.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please put your extra credit poster boards along the wall and then start your Science test.

STUDENT: (Takes an inordinate amount of time putting the poster board along the wall. Sits down, but soon gets up to use the restroom, spending two minutes rearranging the keys on the hooks before leaving.)

TUTOR: Make sure you sign back in and continue your test.

STUDENT: (Spends a minute reading the time on the clock to sign in, takes a circuitous route back to her desk, marks one answer, and then starts writing on her hand.)

TUTOR: Who is still working on their test? Please focus on finishing.

STUDENT: (Goes across the room to wash her hands at the sink for two minutes. After returning, she starts marking her knee and turns around to watch the girls behind her whispering and trying on each other's glasses.)
