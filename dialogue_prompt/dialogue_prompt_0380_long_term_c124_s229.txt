SCENARIO

The student is participating in a small group music session and is asked to try playing a new rhythm on a drum.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Today we are going to try a new music activity with the group. Instead of our usual song, I want you to try playing this new rhythm on the drum.

STUDENT: I don't want to try the new rhythm. I am happy doing what I am used to doing.

TUTOR: Just give the new rhythm a try with the rest of the group.

STUDENT: (Hits the drum off-beat) That was wrong. It did not turn out as expected.

TUTOR: It's fine, just keep playing along with us.

STUDENT: I am very worried. The mistake needs to be corrected immediately. I am unable to think about anything else and I will not try this new thing anymore.
