SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Aiden and Ariel are transitioning to the morning meeting rug at the start of the school day.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Aiden, I saw you walk up and push Ariel. What is going on?

STUDENT: (Aiden makes eye contact) I want him to move. That’s my spot.

TUTOR: Can you use your words to ask him that, to ask him to move somewhere different?

STUDENT: (Aiden looks to the floor, knotting his fingers and kicking the ground) Can you move to another spot? That’s my spot. (Ariel kicks the ground as well and tucks both hands beneath his waistband.)

TUTOR: Did it bother you, Ariel, that Aiden touched you? Did it bother you when he pushed you instead of using words?

STUDENT: (Ariel stares, expressionless. He continues staring, and eventually whispers) I’m sorry that you bothered me.
