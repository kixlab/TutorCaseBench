DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Molly, it is time for lunch. Go ahead and sit at the table with the other students and get your food out.

STUDENT: Please just sit down and start eating your food.

TUTOR: Do you need something to eat?

STUDENT: (Reaches across the table and grabs a classmate's lunch item without asking) Do you need something to eat?
