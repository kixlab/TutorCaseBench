──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The class is participating in a guessing game during morning meeting where the teacher describes a student for the others to identify.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's play our guessing game. I am thinking of someone. His family is from Cameroon, he has a twin brother, and he likes math materials. Is it Bernie?

STUDENT: (Grins enormously, nods, and bends and straightens twice in a row.)

TUTOR: Is your family from Cameroon, Bernie? What part of the world is that in?

STUDENT: (Stands rigidly and does not answer; he is no longer smiling. Looks up and then down to his feet.) I have a rocket at home.

TUTOR: Here it is on the globe. But at choice time, you like to play with math materials. You love them. Did you know that about yourself?

STUDENT: (Turns toward the rest of the class.) I’m gonna blast off and go out of my house. I’m going to knock down my house with that rocket ship. No, I love ice cream! Ice cream cones! I went to the store to buy giant watermelons!
