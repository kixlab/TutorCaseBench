DIALOGUE

TUTOR: We need to select a self-monitoring tool to track your behavior and decide on a goal for your classes. Do you want to use the app on your phone or this paper chart?

TUTOR: Okay, we will use the paper chart. What do you think would be an appropriate replacement behavior for you to track?

TUTOR: Alright, being on time. What should our goal be for that?

STUDENT: I want to set a goal of being on time for all of my classes.
