DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our structured activity. Let's finish completing your animal puzzle.

STUDENT: (Picks up the animal puzzle pieces and starts moving them across the table as if they were walking, making corresponding animal sounds.)

TUTOR: We need to finish the puzzle. Put the pieces in the board.

STUDENT: (Stops moving the animals, drops the pieces, and stares blankly at the table, slipping from an object-engaged state to a state of unengagement.)

TUTOR: Look at the puzzle. Keep trying to finish it.

STUDENT: (Remains completely unengaged and passive, not looking at the teacher or the puzzle, demonstrating a lack of joint attention and no engagement with others.)
