SCENARIO

James is sitting on the carpet playing a matching card game with word cards.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's play our matching card game on the carpet. We will take turns finding word cards that go together, like 'side' and 'walk'.

STUDENT: (Jumps onto his knees and grabs two cards that go together) I found a match!

TUTOR: Yes, they go together, but it is not your turn. You have to return the cards to the floor.

STUDENT: (Returns the cards to the floor and crosses his arms)

TUTOR: We need to keep playing. Please sit up and look at the cards.

STUDENT: (Rolls onto his back, keeping his arms crossed, and completely ignores the other students and the game)
