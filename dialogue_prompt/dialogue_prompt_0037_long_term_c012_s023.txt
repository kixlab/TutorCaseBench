DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: For today's assignment, please use the iPod to create a one- to two-minute video narrative describing your life outside of school.

STUDENT: I'm just trying to get through summer school so I can get to high school and work my odd jobs. I use iPods all the time to talk to my friends, but I really don't want to make a video about my life for this class.

STUDENT: Fine. Here is my room. That is my bed. Here is my TV. I play games.

TUTOR: Can you tell me a little more about those things?

STUDENT: No, that's it. I don't see the value of you knowing about my home life. You would probably not understand anyway.
