SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is at the playground during lunch break, expected to wait in line and take turns playing 4-square handball with her peers.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright, it is time for 4-square handball. Go ahead and line up to wait your turn to get into a square.

STUDENT: (Stands far away from the court, looking down and avoiding the group of nine students waiting in line) ...

TUTOR: You need to join the line with the others.

STUDENT: (Steps further back, refusing to look at the kids her own age) I don't want to go over there.

TUTOR: Just go stand with them and wait.

STUDENT: (Turns her back to the court, completely shutting down and refusing to participate) No. I can't speak to them.
