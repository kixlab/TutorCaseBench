SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Trevor and a tutor are in a 1:1 interaction session using a music player to work on communication skills.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our daily music session. Sit down in your chair and we will sing a song.

STUDENT: (Walks over to the music player, picks it up, and pushes it toward the tutor's hands, gesturing for help to turn it on without making eye contact.)

TUTOR: We can't turn the music on yet. You need to sit in your chair and look at me first.

STUDENT: (Drops the music player on the table, turns his back to the tutor, and stares at the wall, completely ignoring the instruction.)

TUTOR: Trevor, listen to me. Come back here and sit down right now so we can start our activity.

STUDENT: (Vocalizes a loud, sharp protest, pushes the chair away forcefully, and continues to actively ignore the adult by walking to the opposite corner of the room.)
