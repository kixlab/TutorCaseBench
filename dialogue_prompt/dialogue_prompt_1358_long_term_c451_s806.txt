──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Jose, it is time for independent work. Please complete this worksheet at your desk.

STUDENT: (Pushes the worksheet aside and starts playing with books and materials on his desk.)

STUDENT: (Leaves his desk and walks across the classroom to sharpen his pencil.)

STUDENT: (Instead of returning to his desk, he moves around the classroom, talking to peers and interrupting other students who are working.)
