SCENARIO

The student is completing an independent seatwork assignment in a general education classroom to summarize the beginning, middle, and ending of a story using a worksheet.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for independent seatwork. Please read the text silently and complete the worksheet to summarize the beginning, middle, and ending of the story.

STUDENT: (Stares at the worksheet, appearing frustrated and confused) I don't get what I'm supposed to write on this.

TUTOR: Just read the text and do your best to fill out the worksheet.

STUDENT: (Turns around in the seat, engaging in non-instructional activities and interfering with others) Hey, what are you drawing over there?

TUTOR: Please stop talking to your neighbor and focus on your independent reading.

STUDENT: (Pushes the worksheet off the desk, becoming completely off task and disruptive to escape the assignment) I'm not doing this stupid worksheet!
