The student is taking a written exam in a general classroom setting.

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take out your paper and begin writing your answers for the exam.

STUDENT: I have a hard time with handwritten exams. Can I type this on the computer instead? That suits me better.

TUTOR: Let's just stick to the paper for today. Do your best to focus on the questions.

STUDENT: Mmm, well some said that I could kind of go to another room and do the assignments, but that didn't provide the right support anyway. I just want to use the computer.

TUTOR: We need to stay in the classroom right now. Just try to ignore the background noise and keep writing.

STUDENT: I can't. I have always found it difficult if there are sounds, for example sounds from fans and air conditioners or such. I can concentrate on the noise from the air conditioner and completely lose concentration of anything else.
