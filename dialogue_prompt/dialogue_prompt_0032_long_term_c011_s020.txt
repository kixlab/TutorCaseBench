DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your written exam. Please read the first question and write down your answer.

STUDENT: (Looks away from the paper with a lack of eye contact) I don't want to do this, I need to go out to the school yard.

TUTOR: I am fine. Please sit back and look at the next question.

STUDENT: (Starts shouting loudly) The giant birds are breaking the windows! They are taking the desks! Shit!
