TUTOR: I have your worksheet right here. Please look at it and start working.

TUTOR: You have a lot of missing answers and mistakes. You need to sit back down and finish the whole page.

STUDENT: (Becomes tearful) I can't do it! I'm just a bad kid! I'm very bad and my mom is going to send me to another country!
