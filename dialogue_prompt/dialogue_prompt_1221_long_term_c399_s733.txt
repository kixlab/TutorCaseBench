SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Dwayne is participating in a collaborative group project in the classroom with a couple of girls who volunteered to work with him.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Dwayne, you are going to work on the assignment with the girls who volunteered to have you in their group. Please get started together.

STUDENT: Alright, I'm taking charge! You two just sit there and I'll tell you exactly what to write down.

TUTOR: Remember that group work means everyone should contribute.

STUDENT: (Speaking very loudly) I am contributing! I'm the one in charge of this group, so they just need to do what I say!

TUTOR: Please lower your voice and try to listen to what your group members want to do.

STUDENT: Oh I didn't know that I was talking that loud! But I'm not going to listen to them, I'm taking over because I know how to do it better!
