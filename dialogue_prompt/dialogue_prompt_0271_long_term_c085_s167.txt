SCENARIO

A new senior learner is at the school for the first time and is expected to adapt to the strict behavioral rules and procedures.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: As a new senior learner, you need to adapt to our rules here and follow the daily procedures.

STUDENT: Because of the way we used to behave at the school I am coming from, I am not used to doing things this way.

TUTOR: You just need to make sure you follow the instructions.

STUDENT: I am coming to this school for the first time and I find it a challenge to adapt to our rules here.

TUTOR: We expect everyone to abide by the procedures.

STUDENT: (Exhibits misbehaviour) I am just going to keep behaving the way I used to behave at my old school.
