SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Angelica is in her reading class practicing for a standardized test by reading past passages and answering multiple-choice questions.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Today we are going to read passages from past standardized tests and answer the multiple choice questions. The goal is making sure we go back to the passage to find the answers.

STUDENT: Great, it's another reading class. I really didn't like to read as much because it would be really boring and stuff.

TUTOR: We need to practice this for an hour every single day. Please go ahead and start reading the first passage.

STUDENT: Every year since third grade I've been in a reading class and I had to read for like an hour every single day. I'm just really bored with it.

TUTOR: Just try to get through it. Look at the first question and see what you think.

STUDENT: (Circles an answer quickly) I would read the stories and I wouldn't pay attention to the stories. And I would read the questions and I would, just from what I remembered, I would pick the answer. And I would never go back to the story.
