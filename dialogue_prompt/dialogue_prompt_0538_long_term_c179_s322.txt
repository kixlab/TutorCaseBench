SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The teacher is leading a ten-minute reading and storytelling lesson about nature while the student is expected to sit at their desk and listen.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are going to spend the next ten minutes reading a story about nature. Please sit at your desk and listen while I read.

STUDENT: (Twists body to face the back of the room, looking away from the learning stimuli for more than 5 seconds) I don't want to hear a story.

TUTOR: Please pay attention.

STUDENT: (Speaks to another student while the teacher talks) This is stupid, let's do something else.

TUTOR: You need to be quiet.

STUDENT: (Stands up and walks away from the desk, with no body part touching the chair for more than 5 seconds, and raises voice beyond average volume) I'm leaving! I'm not doing this!
