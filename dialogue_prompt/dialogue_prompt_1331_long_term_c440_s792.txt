DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Now that I have finished the monologue playing the bully with this scarf, let's discuss his feelings. Why do you think he bullied the other boy?

STUDENT: He was probably feeling lonely. He bullied the other boy in order to show off and gain some friends.

TUTOR: Okay. What do you think happens when the bully gets punished for it?

STUDENT: Nobody likes being alone. We all want to have friends.

TUTOR: Who do you think the bully could talk to about the incident and his feelings?

STUDENT: The only reliable person he can talk to is himself. He can support himself and advise himself on what to do in order not to lose his friends. He shouldn't talk to anyone else about being angry or frustrated.
