SCENARIO

The student is sitting in a circle in a locked classroom during the first session, where the goal is to listen to the facilitator read a short text to initiate a dialogue.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Welcome to our first session. Now that we are all sitting in a circle, I am going to read this short text out loud so we can start our dialogue.

STUDENT: Do we really have to do this? I don't want to listen to a story.

TUTOR: Let's just try to get through the first page together.

STUDENT: (Looks around the room, ignoring the text) This is taking forever. Hey, what are we having for lunch today?

TUTOR: Please focus on the reading for now.

STUDENT: (Turns completely away from the tutor to face another participant) I'm not listening to this. Hey, did you hear what happened in the yard earlier? This is so boring.
