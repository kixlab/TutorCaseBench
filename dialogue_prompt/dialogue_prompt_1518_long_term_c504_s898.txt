SCENARIO

The student is copying a horizontal math sentence from the blackboard into an exercise book to solve it using standard vertical form.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please copy the math sentence from the blackboard into your exercise book and change it to the standard vertical form.

STUDENT: (Slowly and carefully copies the horizontal sentence from the blackboard, but makes a mistake when setting up the numbers in the vertical form) I wrote it.

TUTOR: Okay, now go ahead and find the total.

STUDENT: (Computes the numerals in the ones column) Eight and four makes twelve. (Writes the '1' in the ones place and carries the '2' to the tens column) Finished.

TUTOR: Please check your answer to make sure it is correct.

STUDENT: (Looks quietly at the exercise book for a moment, reviewing the carried '2' and the '1' in the ones place) I checked it. It is correct.
