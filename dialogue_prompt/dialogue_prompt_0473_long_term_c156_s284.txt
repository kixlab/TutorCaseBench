SCENARIO

The student is discussing a recent classroom interaction with the teacher to learn how to coexist with classmates who have mental health disorders.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: I want to talk about what happened during the group activity today. I noticed there was some trouble.

STUDENT: Yeah, there was some trouble between some kids in my class because of insults. Alex was being disruptive and ruining our game.

TUTOR: What did you and the other kids do when that happened?

STUDENT: Well, in our school there are kids with disorders, and when he started acting weird, some other kids imitated him. If I did that, I would be punished!

TUTOR: Why do you think the other kids were imitating him?

STUDENT: Because it's not fair! If I acted like him, I'd be punished. I know sometimes people can be really horrible to them and they can’t defend themselves on their own, but I'm going to keep insulting and imitating him until he gets in trouble too.
