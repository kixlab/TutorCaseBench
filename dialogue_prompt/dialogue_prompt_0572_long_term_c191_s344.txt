DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: For this assignment, you need to choose which book you want to read and decide how you will present your report.

STUDENT: Which book am I supposed to read? Just tell me which one to pick.

TUTOR: It's your choice. What do you think would be best?

STUDENT: I don't know. I am waiting for you to tell me what I should do.

TUTOR: Take some time to look at the options and make a decision.

STUDENT: This is too difficult. I can't do the work if you won't just give me the instructions and tell me exactly what to do.
