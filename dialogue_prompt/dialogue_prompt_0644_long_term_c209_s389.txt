SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Shen is standing in front of the classroom to present a PowerPoint about his vacation to Massachusetts, New York, and Pennsylvania.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Shen, please begin your PowerPoint presentation about your vacation to Massachusetts, New York, and Pennsylvania.

STUDENT: (Stands by the screen with his pictures, looking a little bit shy and nervous.)

TUTOR: Go ahead and read the labels on your slides for the class.

STUDENT: (Uses a little English) Road trip... horse-riding... (Stops and looks surprised that the teacher is not helping him in his native language.)

TUTOR: What else happened on the trip? Keep trying.

STUDENT: (Becomes very shy and nervous. He looks surprised by the situation since no one is communicating with him in his native language, and he completely stops presenting the highlights of his trip.)
