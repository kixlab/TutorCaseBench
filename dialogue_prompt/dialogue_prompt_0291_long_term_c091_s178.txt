SCENARIO

Hannah and a tutor are in an individual work session looking at a book with lift and reveal flaps.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's sit down and look at this lift and reveal flap book together.

STUDENT: (Takes the book and begins looking rapidly through it independently, appearing to be absorbed in it.)

TUTOR: Let me help you turn the page and see what is under this flap.

STUDENT: (Turns away from the adult and pushes the adult's hand away from the book.)

TUTOR: We need to read this together. Look at this page.

STUDENT: (Moves away, pushes the adult away, and goes back to spending time looking at the book independently to withdraw from the attempt to interact.)
