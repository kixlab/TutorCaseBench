SCENARIO

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take out your physical binder and notepaper so we can organize your research and structure your writing for the short biography.

STUDENT: (Flipping through the binder) I don't know where my research is. I can't find the pages from yesterday.

TUTOR: Well, you need to finish the assignment. Take out a new piece of notepaper and start over.

STUDENT: (Quietly puts his head on his desk, becoming 'invisible') I'm not doing it. I'm just going to lose it again anyway.
