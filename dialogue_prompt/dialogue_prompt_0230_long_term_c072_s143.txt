──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at this storybook together. Look at this page.

STUDENT: (Turns away from the book and the staff member.)

TUTOR: Okay, let's try something else. Look at this toy snake. (Dances the toy snake in front of him)

STUDENT: (Turns away, his mouth opens wide, eyes close, looking alarmed.)

TUTOR: How about the toy tiger instead? (Dances the toy tiger in front of him)

STUDENT: (His hand comes up to ward off the tiger as he screams.)
