DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Let's continue working on your poem for the poetry slam. What is the main topic you are focusing on today?

TUTOR: I see. What else do you want to include in the poem about your experiences?

STUDENT: Maybe about school. I like getting learning support cuz it helps—because it helps me, but I've been thinking about high school.

TUTOR: What are your thoughts about high school?

STUDENT: I don’t want it when I get to high school because I want a new reputation. Like, everybody else already knows that I get learning support and all that. But when I get to high school, it’s gonna be worse, like people are gonna act differently, people are gonna be rude and—I don’t need everybody in my business, and I don’t need people judging me.
