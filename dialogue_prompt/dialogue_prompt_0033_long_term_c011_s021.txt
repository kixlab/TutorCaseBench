DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for your snack. We do not have your regular juice today, so please drink this different juice instead.

STUDENT: (Spins a straw in one hand while holding a toy in the other hand) No, I drink the same juice every day.

TUTOR: You need to drink this one today. Go ahead and try it.

STUDENT: (Starts to cry) No! I eat the same food and drink the same juice every day at school!

TUTOR: We don't have it right now. Please stop crying and sit at your desk.

STUDENT: (Cries uncontrollably and starts screaming. He throws himself on the floor and begins punching his head.)
