SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The Poverty ethnography group is presenting their field research on homelessness to the class during the first small-group presentations of the year.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's begin our first small-group presentations of the year. Poverty ethnography group, please share your field research on homelessness and describe the participants you observed.

STUDENT: For our ethnography, we focused on homelessness. We went out and interviewed some men who were camped underneath a bridge over a dry desert wash.

TUTOR: Thank you for sharing that. What did you learn from your interviews with them?

STUDENT: They shared some really earnest stories with us. They talked a lot about their hunger and how they constantly face harassment by passers-by while they are just trying to survive.

TUTOR: I see. And how did your group decide to conclude your field research for the day?

STUDENT: Hearing their stories compelled us to act immediately. We actually brought the men to a nearby fast food restaurant and paid for their meal to help them right then and there.
