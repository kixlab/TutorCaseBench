SCENARIO

DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (The students remain very quiet, as they are not used to talking in class.)

TUTOR: What do you think the difference is?

STUDENT: (The students look puzzled by the question and stay completely silent, unsure of how to explain the difference between the abstract symbols.)
