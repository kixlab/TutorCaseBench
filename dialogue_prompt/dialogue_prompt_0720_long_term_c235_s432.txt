SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Juan is working on a writing assignment in a small group setting and is asked to edit his story.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please write down your ideas for the story on your sheet of paper, and then review it to see what changes you can make.

STUDENT: (Quickly scribbles a few words on the paper) Done! I finished it already.

TUTOR: Let's look at your story. What can you add to make the ideas more cohesive?

STUDENT: (Erasing a word and rewriting it) I’ve seen my writing... My penmanship is not so good. I need to practice it.

TUTOR: We can worry about the handwriting later. Let's focus on adding more sentences to the story right now.

STUDENT: But Pedro always gets A’s for penmanship on his report card... I want to get A’s, too. I don't need to add more, I just need to make these letters look neater.
