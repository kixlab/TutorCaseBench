SCENARIO

The student is in a regular inclusive classroom listening to the teacher's instructions on what materials to take out for an assignment.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright everyone, let's get started on the new assignment. Look up here at the front to see what materials you need to take out.

STUDENT: I don’t know where to start. The teacher needs to tell me exactly what to take out and what to do.

TUTOR: Just look here at the example I'm holding up, it shows everything you need.

STUDENT: They tell everyone to ‘look here’ and ‘look there’… but I can’t look so I don’t understand what they mean.

TUTOR: We need to keep moving, just try your best to get your things ready.

STUDENT: It’s bad when they say that! Things are going too fast and I still don't know what to take out or what to do.
