──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting at a table with peers during a writing workshop, tasked with completing an independent writing activity about what his name means to him.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our independent writing activity. Please sit at the table and start writing your story about what your name means to you.

STUDENT: I dunno what to write. I’m not a good writer, you know?

TUTOR: You just need to concentrate and focus on your work right now.

STUDENT: (Pretending to be a teacher, using a woman's voice) Don’t make me do the same thing that Ms. Jones did. “Make your story! Make your story!” ((turns the pages)). These are the rules. Don’t tell me you can’t write all the way to the end. Good writers write all the way to the end.

TUTOR: Please stop talking and write your sentences down on the paper.

STUDENT: (Looks blankly for several seconds, then runs around the table reaching out to his peers and almost touching them) Got you! You’re it! You can’t get me!
