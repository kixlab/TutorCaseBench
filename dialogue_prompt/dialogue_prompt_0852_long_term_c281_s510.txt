──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are going to read the next story from the required curriculum. Please get your supplies out and begin reading.

STUDENT: (Keeps eyes downcast curtained by hair) I don't have any supplies for the first day of school.

TUTOR: You can borrow this pencil. Please look at the story.

STUDENT: (Looks confused) It's boring. I'm worried that I won't be able to focus on reading a book.

TUTOR: Just try to pay attention and keep reading.

STUDENT: (Puts head on the desk, then leaves their seat for the fourth time in 10 minutes to sharpen the pencil they borrowed, 'accidentally' hitting someone on the way)
