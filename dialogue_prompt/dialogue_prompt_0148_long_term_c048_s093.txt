SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student and a peer are in a play-session with plastic tracks and a motor car to practice unstructured constructive play.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here are the plastic tracks and the motor car. You and your classmate can play with them now.

STUDENT: (Grabs the motor car away from the peer without speaking and stares at the spinning wheels, ignoring the tracks.)

TUTOR: You need to build the track with your partner.

STUDENT: (Drops the car, looks around the room, and starts wandering away from the play area instead of helping plan the track.)

TUTOR: Come back and figure out how to put the pieces together.

STUDENT: (Suddenly runs toward the door to leave the room, ignoring the peer and the activity completely.)
