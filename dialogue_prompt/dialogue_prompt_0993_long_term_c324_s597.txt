DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our writing workshop. Please start your independent writing on your blank sheet of paper.

STUDENT: (Sits staring at the blank sheet of paper with a worried look, almost a scowl. Writes a word or two and then erases them.)

TUTOR: You need to keep writing. What do you think you should write next?

STUDENT: (Turns her head away and shrinks in her seat, not speaking a word.)

TUTOR: Let me help you get started. (Approaches and touches her on the shoulder)

STUDENT: (Moans with apparent anxiety, turns her head, and shrinks further in her seat.)
