──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

James is sitting at his desk in the pull-out special education classroom working on an independent reading worksheet while other students work nearby.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your independent reading worksheet, James. Please complete these questions quietly at your desk.

STUDENT: (Pushes the worksheet away) I'm not doing this. It's too hard.

TUTOR: Just try your best and see what you can do.

STUDENT: (Starts tapping his pencil loudly on the desk and looks away from the paper) I said I'm not doing it!

TUTOR: Please keep your voice down and focus on your own paper.

STUDENT: (Stands up and shouts across the room) HEY! Look at me! This reading is stupid! (Reaches over and grabs the worksheet off the desk of the student sitting next to him)
