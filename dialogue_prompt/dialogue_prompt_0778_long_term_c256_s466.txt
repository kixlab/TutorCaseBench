DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take your seat. We are starting the lesson, so take out your textbook and the assignment from yesterday.

STUDENT: I don't have my book. The hallways... the smells out there made me dizzy and weak... sick to my stomach almost.

TUTOR: We need the book for today's lesson. What about the assignment?

STUDENT: I only have part of it done. Everything is just cloudy upstairs today. It takes all my effort just remembering what room it is in and going.

TUTOR: Oh, Natasha, you chose to join us today. We expect you to be prepared when you do.

STUDENT: (Grabs her bag and turns to leave the classroom) I can't do this. When you say things like that, you just don't want to go back after that.
