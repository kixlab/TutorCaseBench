──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Derek is sitting in his general English class and is expected to complete his classwork and homework assignments.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Derek, please take out the materials from yesterday's lesson and start working on the classwork assignment.

STUDENT: (Slouching down and crossing his arms across his chest) I’m a different type of student, I think because I have changed over the years and I do my work but at the same time I don’t do anything in class.

TUTOR: You just need to try to remember what we covered yesterday and answer the questions on the page.

STUDENT: I’m really difficult, um, there are a number of things that aren’t easy. Um for some reason I can remember like how to make everything at work but I can’t remember how to do a math problem to save my life, let alone remember this lesson.

TUTOR: Just look at the assignment and do the best you can to get it done.

STUDENT: (Looking completely detached) I'm not investing any time into this. It doesn't matter anyway, it's just like when I got in trouble and kicked out of my last school.
