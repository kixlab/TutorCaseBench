DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Good morning Brian. Let's look at your printed behavior contract and agree on the targets for today. You need to work successfully through five academic programs before you can access the computer for 30 minutes.

TUTOR: Okay, let's start the first academic program.

STUDENT: (Leaves his table without permission and hits the teacher) I don't want to do the programs!
