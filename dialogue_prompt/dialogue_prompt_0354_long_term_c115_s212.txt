DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's write your version of the Brown Bear story on the iPad. Press the button to dictate your sentence about the Transformer.

STUDENT: (Touches the dictation button and goes blank) I want to write... um...

STUDENT: (Repeats the process three times) It typed the wrong words again! (Deletes the entire sentence) I don't want to do this anymore.
