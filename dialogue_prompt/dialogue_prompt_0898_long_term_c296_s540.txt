SCENARIO

The student is participating in a small-group discussion to predict which cup of water will make an egg float and explain the mass differences between the cups.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We have two cups of water and an egg. Which cup do you predict will make the egg float?

STUDENT: I think that this one because of the salt water, it will hold the egg up.

TUTOR: Let's look at the cups on the scale. What do you observe?

STUDENT: This has two things: salt and water. And that one has just one thing.

TUTOR: Notice that both cups have equal volume, but different weights. Why do you think that is?

STUDENT: That’s because it has an extra thing. It makes it heavier.
