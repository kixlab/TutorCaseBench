SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Philip is in his classroom during a language lesson and is asked to complete a written exercise.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Class, it is time for our language lesson. Please look at the board and write the language exercise in your notebooks.

STUDENT: (Leaves his seat without permission and wanders over to another student's desk) I'm not doing that.

TUTOR: Philip, you need to go back to your place and do your work.

STUDENT: (Interrupts the lesson speaking out of turn and annoys his classmates) No! I don't have to listen to you!

TUTOR: (Walks over and tries to drive him to his seat) Come on, let's go back to your seat and sit down.

STUDENT: (Falls on the ground, cries, and refuses to stand up. Grabs a nearby object and tries to throw it, insulting the teacher) Get away from me, you idiot! I hate this!
