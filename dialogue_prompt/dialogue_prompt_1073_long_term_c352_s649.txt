SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Philip is in his English language classroom and is asked to read his homework.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright class, it is time for our English language lesson. Please take out your notebooks so we can read your homework.

STUDENT: (Interrupts the lesson speaking out of turn) I am not reading that!

TUTOR: Philip, it is time to read. Please look at your assignment.

STUDENT: (Leaves his seat without permission and moves to another place in the classroom) No!

TUTOR: (Steps forward to drive him to his seat) Philip, you need to come back and sit in your chair right now.

STUDENT: (Runs around the classroom while singing) La la la! (Falls on the ground and cries as he attempts to escape from the teacher)
