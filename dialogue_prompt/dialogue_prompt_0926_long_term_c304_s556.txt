STUDENT: I don't even know what to do. I was feeling angry all the time and I can't concentrate on this.

TUTOR: Please ignore them and get back to your own work.

STUDENT: (Throws the worksheet off the desk and starts talking loudly to the peers) No! I would rather disrupt than do this. You're all winding me up!
