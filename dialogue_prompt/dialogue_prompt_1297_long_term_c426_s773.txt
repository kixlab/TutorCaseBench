SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Jose is in the preschool classroom and needs to stop his current activity to move toward the next planned activity.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Jose, it is time to stop your current activity and move to the next activity.

STUDENT: (Remains calm and deeply engaged in his activity, not stopping to move toward the next activity.)

TUTOR: Please stop what you are doing and walk over here.

STUDENT: (Continues his activity without stopping, taking a long time to begin the transition.)

TUTOR: Jose, this is a verbal reminder that you need to move to the next activity now.

STUDENT: (Gets upset at the verbal reminder and does not stop his current activity, making the transition take even longer.)
