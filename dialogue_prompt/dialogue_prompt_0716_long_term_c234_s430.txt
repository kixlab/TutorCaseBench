DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Please sit at your desk and complete the math and spelling worksheet. You have 15 minutes to finish.

STUDENT: (Constantly shifting in his place and fidgeting with his stationery items) I do not like written work.

TUTOR: You need to sit down. Your work is incomplete and you are not keeping pace with the other students.

STUDENT: (Pouts and starts crying easily) I can't do it! (Leaves the incomplete work on the desk, looking easily irritated and frustrated)
