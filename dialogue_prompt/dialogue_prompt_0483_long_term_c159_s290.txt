DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the pile of papers and the envelopes. Please start stuffing the envelopes.

STUDENT: (Sits quietly at the desk, looking at the envelopes without touching them as a peer across the room begins making loud, repetitive moaning noises.)

TUTOR: Go ahead and put the papers in.

STUDENT: (Picks up one paper but stops, turning their head to passively watch the other activities and instructional routines happening in the classroom.)

TUTOR: Keep working on those. Anyway, as I was saying to the other teacher, the schedule for tomorrow is completely changed...

STUDENT: (Drops the paper back onto the desk and sits completely still, demonstrating no competing behavior but remaining entirely disengaged from the task as the adults continue their off-task conversation.)
