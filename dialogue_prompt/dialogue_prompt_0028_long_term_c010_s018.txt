DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (Focuses on the objects with irregular hand movements. He moves his hand as if about to put a checkmark on the pencil and cap, but hesitates and turns his attention to the tissue box) Tissue... tissue...

TUTOR: What about the ball? Can you find the ball?

STUDENT: (He ignores the image of the marbles. His hand movements give a checkmark on the picture of the hat instead. He stares at the canned milk and repeats quickly) Canned milk, canned milk.
