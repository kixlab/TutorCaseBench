DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Chris, playtime is over. It is time to put the toys away and stop playing.

STUDENT: (Elopes from the activity area, running away from the toys) No!

STUDENT: (Grabs an item from a nearby table, throws it across the classroom, and starts kicking) No!
