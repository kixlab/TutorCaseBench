──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Robert is in his second-grade classroom and is expected to complete a math worksheet at his desk.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to work on our math worksheets. Please sit at your desk and get started.

STUDENT: (Crawls under the desk instead of sitting in the chair) I don't want to do math today!

TUTOR: Robert, you need to come out from under the desk and do your work.

STUDENT: (Slides out from under the desk, stands up, and starts hopping around the aisle) No! I'm not sitting down!

TUTOR: Stop hopping right now. Sit in your chair.

STUDENT: (Stops hopping, balls his fists, and steps aggressively toward the teacher) Make me! I'll hit you if you try to make me sit down!
