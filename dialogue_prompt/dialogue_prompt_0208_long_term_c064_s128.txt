SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is in the classroom during a lesson and is expected to turn in homework and participate in class activities.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take out your homework assignments so we can start the lesson.

STUDENT: I didn't do it. I remember having a hard time learning things, especially maths, which I still struggle with.

TUTOR: Why did you not do your homework? You need to get to grips with schoolwork.

STUDENT: Because I just felt so depressed, that I just sat there even though I didn’t want to and couldn’t stand it, I just sat there, just to try to make time pass.

TUTOR: You can't just sit there quietly during the lessons. You need to participate and try working with your classmates.

STUDENT: I’ve never felt that I belonged anywhere or with classmates. I didn’t feel like a proper human being. It feels as if you’re lying on the ground and everybody’s just stepping on you.
