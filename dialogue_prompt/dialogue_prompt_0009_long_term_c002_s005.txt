SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Sam is working one-on-one with an interventionist to sound out words on treasure hunt clues to find a prize.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the first clue for our treasure hunt. Let's sound out these words to find out where the prize is.

STUDENT: (Gets out of his chair and begins roaming the classroom instead of looking at the clue.)

TUTOR: Please come back to your seat and look at the paper.

STUDENT: No! I don't want to read it.

TUTOR: We need to read the sentence to get the next clue.

STUDENT: (Throws the clue on the floor and begins having a meltdown, screaming and completely refusing to participate in the instructional task.)
