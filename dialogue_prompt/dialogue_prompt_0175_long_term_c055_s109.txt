SCENARIO

The student is placing a cross on an emotion chart to reflect on their weekend and is being asked to explain their choice to the class.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Good morning. Please take a red or green cross and place it on the emotion chart to show how you felt over the weekend.

STUDENT: (Places a red cross on the chart but stays silent, looking extremely shy.)

TUTOR: Now, can you justify your choice to the rest of the class and explain why you felt that way?

STUDENT: (Struggles to speak to the other kids in the class) I don't know.

TUTOR: Just try to verbalise your feelings. How might you change the circumstances if you were in that situation again?

STUDENT: I don't care. I'm never going to be more capable at this anyway.
