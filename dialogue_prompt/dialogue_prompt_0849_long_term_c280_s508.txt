SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Jamal is sitting at his desk in a large classroom and is asked to write the basic alphabet on his paper.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Jamal, it is time to practice our letters. Please take your pencil and write the basic alphabet on your paper.

STUDENT: (Pouts his lips) I can't see! My foot hurts...

TUTOR: You can see fine from your desk. Just start with the letter A.

STUDENT: I have to go to the bathroom! My pencil broke!

TUTOR: We need to finish writing the alphabet first, then you can go.

STUDENT: (Starts crying and kicks at his desk) I can't do it! (Tears his paper)
