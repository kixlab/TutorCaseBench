DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Class, please focus on answering the questions correctly on your worksheet. I am giving a verbal warning to anyone not attending to the lesson.

STUDENT: (Stops talking and sits quietly, but does not work hard on the questions.)

TUTOR: You haven't written anything. If you don't complete this, you will be disallowed from going on the school trip.

STUDENT: (Does even less work, pushing the paper away.)

TUTOR: I am telling you off in front of the class right now. You need to pick up your pen and work.

STUDENT: (Stays completely quiet and withdraws, showing their response to the teacher is at stake due to the public admonishment.)
