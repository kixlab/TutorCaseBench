SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Farid is working one-on-one with his teacher to transcribe the letter 't' from lower-case to upper-case on a worksheet using an alphabet table.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Farid, please find the lower-case letter 't' on the alphabet table.

STUDENT: (Hesitates, staring at the alphabet table without pointing to a letter.)

TUTOR: Keep looking. Once you find it, copy the upper-case version of it into the blank space on your worksheet.

STUDENT: (Finds the letter, but instead of copying the upper-case 'T', he replicates the lower-case form in the blank space.)

TUTOR: That is not correct. Look in the first line of the alphabet table again and write the upper-case letter in the box.

STUDENT: (Locates the lower-case 't' on the table and again copies the lower-case 't' into the box.)
