Chris is sitting in his 50-minute math class with the goal of acquiring new math concepts.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Chris, it is time to start our 50-minute math class. Please get out your pencil, paper, and math book so we can begin.

STUDENT: (Drawing pictures) I don't have my pencil, paper, or math book today.

TUTOR: You need to stop drawing and pay attention to the lesson.

STUDENT: Math is just not my subject. Watch this, I'm going to draw something funny to show the other students.

TUTOR: Please look up here at the board and try to follow along with the concepts.

STUDENT: (Continues doodling, not looking at the teacher) I know that when I listen more I get it more easily, but I'm just going to keep drawing.
