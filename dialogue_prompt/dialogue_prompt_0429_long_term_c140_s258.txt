──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Mariah, it is time to work on the written essay for senior English. Please start writing your analysis of the text.

STUDENT: The text is actually fascinating! The author's use of symbolism really shows the protagonist's isolation. I was just explaining to the group over there how the recurring motif of the ocean represents his feeling of drowning in responsibilities.

TUTOR: That is a good thought. Go ahead and put those ideas on your paper.

STUDENT: Yeah, I'll get to it. But honestly, discussing it is so much better. Hey guys, don't you agree the protagonist is just misunderstood? It's way more interesting to talk about it together.

TUTOR: We have been on this unit for weeks now, and today is the last day I can accept work. You need to submit your written assignment.

STUDENT: (Pulls a wrinkled and stained paper from her bag) Fine, here. I wrote down a few sentences about it. That should be enough for a D so I can pass, right? I have a full-time shift right after this.
