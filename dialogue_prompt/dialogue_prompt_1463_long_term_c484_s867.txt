SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Julia is sitting at the computer in her writing class, tasked with independently revising her draft to add vivid descriptions and details.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Julia, please open your draft on the computer. I want you to revise your work independently by adding some actions, feelings, and precise nouns to make the description better.

STUDENT: (Raises hand immediately) I don't know what to do. I can't get started on this by myself.

TUTOR: Just read what you have so far and try to add some details where you can.

STUDENT: But I feel really challenged by this writing. I don't know how to spell the new words, and my sentences are all messed up.

TUTOR: Keep trying. Just do your best to organize your thoughts on the screen.

STUDENT: (Complaining) I can't organize it! I struggle with the planning and the grammar, and I need you to come over here and help me fix the sentence structure!
