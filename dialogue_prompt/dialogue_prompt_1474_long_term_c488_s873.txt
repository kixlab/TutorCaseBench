SCENARIO

Matthew is using the 'English Alphabet for kids 4' app on an iPad secured to his wheelchair and needs to tap screen icons to progress through the story.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's use the 'English Alphabet for kids 4' app on your iPad. Tap the screen icon to start the story.

STUDENT: (Looks at the iPad secured to the arm of his wheelchair, trying to lift his hand to make a pointing gesture but unable to support its weight.)

TUTOR: Go ahead and tap the icon.

STUDENT: (His hand drops back down, unable to maintain the pointing gesture near the screen independently.)

TUTOR: You have to tap the relevant on-screen icon to make the story progress.

STUDENT: (Watches the story activity unfold on-screen but cannot independently point to and tap the icon without someone supporting the weight of his hand.)
