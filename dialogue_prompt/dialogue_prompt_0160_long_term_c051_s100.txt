STUDENT: (Evie uses eye gaze to look at the materials, then makes a reaching motion toward the adult while making a differentiated vocalization.)

STUDENT: (Evie becomes completely disengaged from the activity, turning her head away and escalating to crying, showing she is unhappy and having difficulty expressing herself.)
