SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Martin is participating in a small-group reading activity in his second-grade classroom.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's begin our small-group reading. Please open your books and read the next page quietly.

STUDENT: (Turns his body completely away from the assigned materials) I already know how to read. I'm not doing it.

TUTOR: Just do your best to read along with the group.

STUDENT: (Gets out of his seat and leans over to the student next to him) Hey, what are you doing after school today?

TUTOR: Martin, please sit down and focus on your book.

STUDENT: (Stays out of his seat, ignoring the book, and continues talking loudly to his peer) I don't want to read this! Did you see the new superhero movie?
