DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take out your math worksheet so we can continue with today's regular lesson.

STUDENT: I don't want to do math today. I just have this really uneasy feeling about being locked in a psychiatric department, and my motivation is completely gone.

TUTOR: Let's just try to get through the first few equations on the page.

STUDENT: No, I can't! My weekly schedule was changed this morning and everything has to be exactly the way I planned it or I can't function.

TUTOR: You need to cooperate and finish the work that was assigned to you.

STUDENT: (Sweeps the math worksheet and pencils violently off the desk) I said I'm not doing it! Leave me alone!
