Philip is in the school yard during break time while his classmates are starting a game of soccer.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: The bell just rang for break time. You can go out to the school yard now.

STUDENT: (Follows the classmates outside and intrudes on their soccer game, trying to grab the ball to get their attention)

TUTOR: Philip, please let them play their game.

STUDENT: But I want to play! (Stands in the middle of the field, annoying the other children)

TUTOR: You need to figure out how to join in with them.

STUDENT: (Falls on the ground) You didn't invite me to play with you! (Cries loudly and yells at his classmates)
