SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting in a circle with peers during a group philosophical inquiry session and is asked to respond to a point made by his sibling class-mate about a stimulus passage.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are sitting in our circle for today's discussion. Your sister just shared her idea about the passage. What are your thoughts on what she said?

STUDENT: (Tapping his chair loudly) I don't know. What she said is just wrong.

TUTOR: Why do you think it's wrong? Go ahead and explain your point to the group.

STUDENT: Because... it's just... sometimes it’s really difficult, you need to follow the story and you need to think about what the person’s saying... and I can't figure out how to say it!

TUTOR: Take your time and just tell us what you are thinking.

STUDENT: (Stands up abruptly) I can't organize my thoughts! I'm getting angry about it and I can't cope right now, I'm leaving the session!
