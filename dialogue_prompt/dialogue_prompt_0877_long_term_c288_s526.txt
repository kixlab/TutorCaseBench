──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please self-evaluate your writing draft using the correction goal sheet and the four-point Likert scale, where 0 means 'need help with this' and 3 means 'personal best'.

STUDENT: I don't know how to grade my own work. I took an AP English class and that teacher never told me what I was doing wrong. She would just say I got an F.

TUTOR: Just look at what you wrote and try to pick a number on the scale.

STUDENT: I can't. Before this class I thought that I was the worst writer in the world. Without knowing where I did good and where I messed up, I just feel like I'm failing.

TUTOR: You need to finish the evaluation so you can get credit for the draft.

STUDENT: I'm not completing the assignment. I'm just embarrassed by my writing and I've lost hope of ever being successful.
