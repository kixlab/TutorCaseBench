SCENARIO

The class is gathered in the morning meeting circle to select a greeting gesture to use as they go around the circle.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We need to choose a gesture for our morning greeting. Theodore suggested touching knees. What do you think?

STUDENT: (Puts her hand in the air and begins waving it around desperately, jumps up, and bounces on one foot) Neck touches are giraffe kisses.

TUTOR: That sounds like a gentle moment between a mother giraffe and her child.

STUDENT: (Punches a fist into the air) No. That’s not how it goes, it’s not like that.

TUTOR: Can you show us what you mean?

STUDENT: (Raises both arms up, standing again and aggressively knocking her forearms into each other, then punches her fists together, grunting softly and cracking her knuckles) The giraffes are kissing and it’s like this, and they also do this too.
