SCENARIO

The student is working alongside a peer to determine how many 25-seater buses are needed for 100 people.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the next problem for you both. There are 100 people waiting, and they need to fit into 25-seater buses. How many buses will they need?

STUDENT: (Draws a large box on the paper) I'm going to draw the buses and put one mark for each person. Counting them out is the most efficient way for me to do this.

TUTOR: Okay, keep working on it.

STUDENT: (Carefully drawing individual tally marks inside the first container) ...twenty-three, twenty-four, twenty-five. That's one bus. Now I'll draw another bus and add more marks.

TUTOR: Your partner just mentioned that they have finished the task. Just keep going with yours.

STUDENT: (Looks over at the partner's paper and stops drawing) They finished already? I need to go faster. I can't draw all these marks now, it takes too long. I'm stuck.
