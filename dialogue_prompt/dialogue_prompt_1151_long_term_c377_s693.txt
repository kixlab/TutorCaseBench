SCENARIO

Shirley is participating in a cookery task in the classroom and is asked to finish preparing her ingredients within a specific time limit for assessment.

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please finish preparing your ingredients. We have ten minutes left before I come around to assess everyone's cookery tasks in front of the class.

STUDENT: Ten minutes? And you're going to assess us in front of everyone? I am really afraid I won't be able to cope with this kind of pressure.

TUTOR: Just keep working on it and do your best.

STUDENT: I can't take pressure anymore. Having to perform in front of others with a time limit is too much for me.

TUTOR: We need to stay on schedule for the class assessment.

STUDENT: If you put me under any pressure, I will leave. I am packing up my things right now.
