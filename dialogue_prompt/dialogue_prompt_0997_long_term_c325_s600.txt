SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Edward is expected to work on an independent writing assignment during writing workshop.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our writing workshop. Please sit at your desk and begin your independent writing task.

STUDENT: (Loudly) This is boring and I don't want to do it! (He gets out of his seat and wanders toward the back of the classroom, unable to sit still.)

TUTOR: Please go back to your seat and start writing on your paper.

STUDENT: (Sits down, writes a single word, then immediately stands back up) Wait, I have a question about this.

TUTOR: Try to figure it out on your own and keep writing.

STUDENT: (Snaps his pencil point on the desk) Oops, my pencil broke again. I have to go sharpen it. (He walks to the sharpener for the third time in five minutes, leaving his paper mostly blank.)
