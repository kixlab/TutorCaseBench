TUTOR: It is time for sustained silent reading. Please take out your choice book, Monster, and read quietly.

STUDENT: Okay. I picked this one because the story seemed realistic. I used to read pretty slow and not understand the words but now I’m a lot better.

TUTOR: Just keep trying your best to read it.

STUDENT: (Puts the book down and turns to watch the other students in the class) I can't focus when these words are so hard. It just makes reading unappealing, and I'd rather see what they are doing.
