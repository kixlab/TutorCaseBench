DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your preferred item. You can play with it while we sit in the room.

STUDENT: (Ignores the item and crosses her hands tightly across her chest.)

STUDENT: (Uncrosses her hands and begins scratching and pinching her arms, then quickly places her hands behind her back.)

TUTOR: Let's just sit quietly then.

STUDENT: (Begins hitting herself in the head and banging her helmet against the padded wall, then immediately sits on her hands and holds them under her clothing to restrain them.)
