SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Lily is participating in a group story-making activity in the sensory room and is asked to use her communication book to suggest ideas for the drama.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Lily, it is time for our story-making activity in the spinney. What should happen next in our drama? Please use your communication book to show me your idea.

STUDENT: (Looks away from the communication book and keeps her hands in her lap, reluctant to use the aid when asked the direct question.)

TUTOR: What do you think? Just point to something in the book.

STUDENT: No. (Frowns and uses a sad facial expression, refusing to sign or point.)

TUTOR: We need you to suggest an idea for the story. Keep trying.

STUDENT: (Looks visibly cross and pushes the communication book away, remaining completely reluctant to suggest any ideas or make a choice for the story.)
