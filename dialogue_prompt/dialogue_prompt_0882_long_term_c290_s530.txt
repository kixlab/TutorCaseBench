SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting at a table in a preschool special education classroom, tasked with sorting colored blocks into bins.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to sit at the table and sort these colored blocks into the right bins.

STUDENT: (Picks up a red block) Look! This is a fire truck! Wee-woo, wee-woo!

TUTOR: Put the block in the bin.

STUDENT: (Moves the block along the table) The fire truck is driving fast to save the people!

TUTOR: We need to finish sorting the blocks.

STUDENT: (Gathers more blocks together) Now these are the buildings on fire! The truck has to spray water everywhere!
