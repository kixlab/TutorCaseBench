STUDENT: I'm absolutely petrified. Talking and reading about versus doing qualitative research is the same as if I read a book on 'the process of how to successfully catch an Amazon Marmoset', and then someone tossed me into the rain forest expecting me to find my way. My anticipation has melted into fear and doubt, and I can not help but think I am way out of my league.
