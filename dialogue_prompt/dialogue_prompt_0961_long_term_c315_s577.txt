DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: You did well multiplying the last expression with the Algebra Lab Gear. For this next problem, let's move away from the blocks and try to solve it using only abstract symbols on your paper.

STUDENT: But I like using these. I’m writing out my problem over here. I have an equation and I am breaking it up and multiplying because this is a multiplying bar and this is x and so I have x plus 3 so x plus 3 times, and this is timesing it, x plus 2 and that’s going to equal x squared.

TUTOR: That is exactly what you did. Now, let's put the corner piece and the blocks aside and just write the numbers for the next one.

STUDENT: I think if it was broken down I would be able to do it. The problem is that there are a lot of steps and once I understand what to do with one part I forget the other – I guess I’m not good at blending the steps.

TUTOR: Let's just try to write it out with the numbers and see what happens.

STUDENT: It’s so much more hard because it’s not broken up then. Like what I do is I see this (pointing to x-bar) and this (pointing to constant blocks) and I read it and I write it then I move it. And then it’s all broken up and I see the whole problem happening. But when it’s all numbers then I forgot where to break it up and what’s what.
