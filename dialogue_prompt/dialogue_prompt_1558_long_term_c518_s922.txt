DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Joseph, it is time for our transition. Please look at the visual supports to see what we are doing next.

STUDENT: (Displays signs of distress and frustration, pacing away from the visual supports.)

TUTOR: Listen to my verbal prompts, Joseph. You need to calm down and come over here.

STUDENT: (Becomes increasingly agitated as the verbal prompts continue, stepping forward and beginning to scratch Ms. Tracey.)

TUTOR: Stop scratching right now. We need to finish this transition.

STUDENT: (Begins kicking and hitting Mrs. O’Shaughnessy. As she attempts to back away, he grabs a fist-full of her hair and pulls her to the floor.)
