STUDENT: (Pulls his chair into the corner at the back of the room between a table and a cabinet, readjusting his table to have it touch his belly so he sits tall and moves nothing but his feet.) Can I take my test back here?

STUDENT: (Sits still, looks silently at the teacher distributing the test booklets, and then directs his gaze on his own booklet.)
