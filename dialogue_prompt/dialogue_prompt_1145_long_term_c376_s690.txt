Colin is participating in a specialized cookery course where he needs to chop onions and add them to a recipe at the right time.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Colin, please chop the onions and put them in at the right time for the recipe.

STUDENT: I'm not sure when they go in... I have a fear of failure and I'm worried the other students will laugh at me if I make a mistake.

TUTOR: Colin, just follow the instructions on the page.

STUDENT: (Colin looks down at the onions, feeling embarrassment about asking for help. He stares blankly, exhausted from severe insomnia and depression.)

TUTOR: Colin, I am talking to you. Why are you just standing there?

STUDENT: You really showed me up! I didn't realise you were talking to me. That done it for me. I'm leaving and I'm never going back.
