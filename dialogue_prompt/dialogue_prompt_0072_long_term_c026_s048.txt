The student is sitting at the table during school mealtime and needs to request water and food.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for lunch. We are sitting at the table, and your food and water are right here.

STUDENT: (Reaches directly for the water cup, ignoring the PECS symbols on the table, expecting to receive the water without having to ask.)

TUTOR: What do you want? You need to tell me.

STUDENT: (Begins to rock back and forth in the chair, displaying stereotyped behaviors, and makes passive, frustrated vocalizations directed at the water.)

TUTOR: I don't understand what you are trying to say. Just wait a minute.

STUDENT: (Starts crying loudly and aggressively hits the table with both hands because the request for water was not quickly and correctly decoded.)
