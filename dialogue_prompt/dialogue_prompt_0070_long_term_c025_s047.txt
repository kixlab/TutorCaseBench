DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's take some time in our peer fellow session today to reflect on the recent Biology exam results and determine the causes of any difficulties you might have had.

STUDENT: I got my grade back and it's really disappointing. I studied the night before the exam and took breaks while studying, but I guess I can't do this. I'm not a Biology person.

STUDENT: I usually study alone primarily. But really, the professor talks too fast. The test didn't cover the lectures in class.

TUTOR: How do you plan to prepare for the next exam?

STUDENT: She doesn't teach, care, or want us to pass, so I'm not going to. Everyone agrees with me. I'm just not smart enough.
