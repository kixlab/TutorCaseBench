SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Karl and his peer James are standing in the middle of a circle using imaginary swords to act out a scene where Achilles strikes Moulios.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: James and Karl, let's show what 'Achilles struck Moulios on the head with his sword' might have looked like to the observing Greek and Trojan warriors. We're just going to pretend to fight to the death. But no actually touching. OK?

STUDENT: (Agrees, pretends to wield an imaginary sword, and turns toward James) OK.

TUTOR: Go ahead and start the scene.

STUDENT: (Eyes James carefully, looking for a sneaky way to push his buttons before the strike) You're going down.

TUTOR: Let's just focus on acting out the scene.

STUDENT: (As James lurches forward and cries out, 'I cut your head off!') No way! You can't just do that, I wasn't even ready and you stepped too fast, so technically that doesn't count at all!
