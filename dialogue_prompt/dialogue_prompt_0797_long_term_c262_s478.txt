SCENARIO

Sam is in science class and is asked to read a passage from the textbook aloud to study new vocabulary words.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Sam, please read the next paragraph from the science textbook aloud so we can go over the new vocabulary words.

STUDENT: I like science, but I don't want to read the textbook. I'm not reading it out loud.

TUTOR: Just do your best and read the first sentence for us.

STUDENT: (Looks around the room defensively) No! The other students are just going to make fun of me. I know they are!

TUTOR: No one is going to make fun of you. Go ahead and start reading.

STUDENT: (Slams the textbook shut and yells at a nearby peer) What are you looking at?! I'm not reading this stupid book! Leave me alone!
