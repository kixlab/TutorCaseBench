SCENARIO

The student is sitting alone at a desk in a mainstream classroom, tasked with copying trigonometry formulas from the whiteboard to calculate the heights of structures.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please sit quietly at your desk and copy the trigonometry formulas from the whiteboard. We will use these later to find the heights of different structures.

STUDENT: I feel bored all the time sitting down. I prefer standing up and working in groups rather than alone.

TUTOR: We are working individually right now. Please just look at the board and write down the notes.

STUDENT: Mentally I cannot understand this just by looking at text. Pictures make a big difference for me, I wouldn't have understood without them.

TUTOR: You just need to sit still and keep trying to copy what is written.

STUDENT: I can't just be like a statue and copy all the time from the whiteboard and gaze at it! (Turns around and shoves the classmate behind him) Stop kicking my chair, do you want to fight?
