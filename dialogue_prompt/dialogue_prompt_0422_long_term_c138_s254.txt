SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The tutor and student are reviewing written comments on an English essay during a one-on-one meeting.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your essay, Jennifer. Please look over the written comments I left on it so you can revise it for the final draft.

STUDENT: Wait, you actually read my paper? I just figured you would put a grade on it and hand it back.

TUTOR: Yes, I read all the papers. What do you think about the feedback I wrote?

STUDENT: I mean, I got a 95 on it. I'm satisfied with my work as long as my grades are acceptable.

TUTOR: But you could elaborate more and extend your thinking. Don't you want to improve?

STUDENT: I don't see any advantage in making the additional effort to read and apply those comments. I already have a good grade, and I don't see any value in how this task is relevant to my life anyway.
