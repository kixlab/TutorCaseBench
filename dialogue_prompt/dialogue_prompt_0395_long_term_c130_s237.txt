DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Please start your silent reading session and read your book about monkeys.

TUTOR: Keep trying to read it on your own.

STUDENT: (Stops reading) No, if I don't understand any words, you need to explain for me. Then you will do it the same as me. I can't read it until you sit and do it with me.
