SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Dominic is playing at the sensory table with small plastic animals and other children during free choice time.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is free choice time, so you can play at the sensory table with the small plastic animals and your classmates.

STUDENT: (Picks up the plastic animals from the sensory table) This is a bear, roar! And this is a wolf, awooo!

TUTOR: Keep playing with the animals.

STUDENT: (Continues playing for a long period of time, then suddenly moves his animals into another child's space) The bear is coming over here now.

TUTOR: Remember to share the space at the table.

STUDENT: (Hits the child next to him) No, I need those! (Takes the other plastic animals away from the child)
