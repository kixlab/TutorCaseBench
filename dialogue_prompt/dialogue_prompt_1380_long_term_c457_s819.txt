SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Isaac is participating in a morning check-in with his teacher to review his planner and turn in his homework.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Good morning, Isaac. It is time for our morning check-in. Please take out your planner and hand in last night's homework.

STUDENT: I don't have it. I didn't even know what assignment was due.

TUTOR: You need to keep track of your assignments. Look in your backpack and see what materials you brought for class today.

STUDENT: (Digs through his bag) I don't have my materials. I think I actually completed the assignment, but I can't find it anywhere in here.

TUTOR: If you cannot find your work, you will have to sit down and redo the assignment right now.

STUDENT: No! I'm not doing it again! (Pushes his desk away and refuses to open his book)
