DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (Drones the words in a speaking-voice monotone, not matching pitch with her voice) Look who is here.

STUDENT: (Shows a lack of vocal effort and stops singing entirely, appearing aware that the sounds she produces are not the same as the other students around her)
