──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Mike is at his desk in the second-grade classroom during independent writing time, tasked with writing a paragraph containing a starter sentence, detail sentences, and a conclusion statement.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for independent writing. Please begin working on your assignment, making sure to include your starter sentence, detail sentences, and a conclusion statement.

STUDENT: (Mike picks up his pencil and begins loudly tapping it on his desk, looking around the room instead of at his paper.)

TUTOR: Mike, please stop tapping and get to work on your writing.

STUDENT: (Mike gets out of his seat without permission and walks across the room to the pencil sharpener.) I need to sharpen my pencil again.

TUTOR: You just sharpened it. Please sit back down and write your starter sentence.

STUDENT: (Mike walks back to his group but does not sit down. He leans over, tapping the student next to him on the shoulder while they are working, and calls out across the room) Hey, what are you writing? I don't want to do this!
