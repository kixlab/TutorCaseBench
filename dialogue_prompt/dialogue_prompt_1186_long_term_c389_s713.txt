DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to make a play plan for the kitchen toys. What do you want to do with them?

STUDENT: (Stands away from the peer and looks at the floor, appearing lonely and anxious instead of initiating a conversation.)

STUDENT: (Remains completely silent, making no comments to the peer about the toys.)

TUTOR: Come on, you need to make a plan together. Tell each other what to do.

STUDENT: (Shows increased anxiety during the awkward silence and begins to have a temper tantrum, throwing a plastic plate and refusing to follow directions.)
