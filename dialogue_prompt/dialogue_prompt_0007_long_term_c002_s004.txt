Sam and his tutor are working one-on-one in a quiet spot to practice remembering the sounds of previously taught letters when a new letter is introduced.

DIALOGUE

TUTOR: We have a new letter today. But first, let's look at the letter we learned yesterday. What sound does this one make?

STUDENT: (Looks away) I don't want to read.

STUDENT: I don't remember! I'm not doing it!

TUTOR: Try to remember. We need to finish this instructional task.

STUDENT: No! (Gets out of his chair and begins roaming the classroom, screaming and starting to have a meltdown)
