SCENARIO

The student is in a small group intervention block tasked with creating an after-school schedule to learn about managing time.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Today we are learning about Managing Your Time. Please use this paper to create a schedule for your time after school.

STUDENT: I don't need to make a schedule. I already know how to do my reading and homework. I don't even like being in this small group.

TUTOR: Just try your best to write down a few things you do when you get home.

STUDENT: (Pushes the paper away) I'm not writing it. I'm already smart enough and I'm missing my regular class for this.

TUTOR: We need to finish this activity before the session is over. Please focus on the paper.

STUDENT: (Stares at the wall, completely off-task) I don't care. I'm not doing this assignment. This group is a waste of time and I'm not writing anything down.
