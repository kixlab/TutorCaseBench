DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Please read this assigned text and answer the comprehension questions. You have 20 minutes to finish.

STUDENT: I am an avid reader who enjoys reading when it’s not required. When it’s required, I think that it just feels like a chore, something I have to do, and I don’t want to do it.

TUTOR: We need to practice reading quickly and ripping apart the text to find the meaning.

STUDENT: I’m a really slow reader, and I have a lot of issues with reading, so when I have a specific time, it puts more pressure on me and makes it a lot more difficult for me to read.

TUTOR: Just try your best to get through the questions.

STUDENT: (Refuses to complete the required reading) The reading parts, I suffer in ‘cause I just don’t like school reading. I never read for English.
