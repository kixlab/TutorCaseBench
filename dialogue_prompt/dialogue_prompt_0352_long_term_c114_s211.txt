The student is playing the x20 mental math game on a Nintendo DS during a daily 20-minute session.

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for your 20-minute Nintendo DS session. Please look at the first math problem on the screen.

STUDENT: (Looks at the screen and then turns to the classmate sitting next to her) I know that is a plus and a times sign, but I don't know what to do. Hey, what did you get for this one?

TUTOR: Please focus on your own screen and try to work it out.

STUDENT: (Quickly taps a random number on the DS screen) I'll just put this.

TUTOR: That is not the right answer. Look at the symbols again and try one more time.

STUDENT: (Puts the Nintendo DS down on the desk, crosses her arms, and stares blankly at the screen without saying anything or asking for help)
