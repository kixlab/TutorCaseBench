SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is interacting with a teacher regarding missing independent practice and homework assignments.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: I noticed you didn't submit your independent practice today. You need to turn it in.

STUDENT: (Looks away) I didn't do it.

TUTOR: Well, you need to complete it. Just get it done and turn it in tomorrow.

STUDENT: Elementary school was easier, and I didn't get as distracted. My teachers knew me better, and they found out what I liked, and they don't do that in middle school.

TUTOR: You are in middle school now, so you just need to do the work. Do you have any questions about the assignment?

STUDENT: (Shakes head, refusing to ask questions out of fear of embarrassment) No. But if I don't do the homework, it means I am having trouble or I didn't understand the assignment. You just tell me to turn it in instead of asking if I'm having difficulties.
