SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is expected to sit and read silently while the teacher gives instructions to the class.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright class, please stay seated and read the story silently while I go over the instructions for our next activity.

STUDENT: (Sits with a dreamy and glazed look, not opening the book and not responding immediately to the instructions.)

TUTOR: Did you hear me? It is time to read silently.

STUDENT: (Stands up and starts walking around the classroom when staying seated is expected, talking out of turn.) I don't want to read right now.

TUTOR: Please go back to your seat and listen to the instructions.

STUDENT: (Interrupts the teacher's instruction, walks to a peer's desk, takes away their stationery items, and starts making noises and arguing with the peer.) No, I need this pencil! Vroom!
