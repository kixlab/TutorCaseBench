SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Amy and a staff member are sitting together to complete behavioral reflection worksheets during a pull-out session.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We need to complete this 'Thinking about My Behavior' worksheet about what happened yesterday.

STUDENT: I don't want to do it. I've been 'getting in trouble' for as long as I can remember, and this won't change anything.

TUTOR: Please just answer the first question on the paper.

STUDENT: I already told you what happened. I felt out of control and I got angry.

TUTOR: Write that down on the 'Understanding Feelings Can Affect My Behavior' page.

STUDENT: I said I'm not doing it! You better leave me alone or I'll hit you, just like I hit that kid in the after-school program!
