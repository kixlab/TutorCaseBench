SCENARIO

The student is sitting on the carpet after lunch participating in a whole-class read-aloud discussion of Ereth's Birthday.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's start our read-aloud of Ereth's Birthday. Before we read the next page, Roger was just saying he didn't think the fisher deserved to get in that trap anyway.

STUDENT: He didn't get killed.

TUTOR: Let's remember to raise our hands. In this next part of the story, Ereth calls someone a 'loudmouth lollie'.

STUDENT: (Fidgeting and facing away) Loudmouth lollie! (Guffaws and looks around at the other students, making a funny face)

TUTOR: Please sit still and listen. Now, what do you call it when an animal acts like a human—has human qualities like talking or playing music?

STUDENT: (In a sassy tone) Um, unnecessary? (Laughs loudly and makes another silly noise, hoping to enlist others in the silliness)
