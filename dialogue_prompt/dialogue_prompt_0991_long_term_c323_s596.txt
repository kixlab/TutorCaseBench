DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's open your binder and start on today's core academic assignment. You need to complete all these problems because everything you learn in here you're going to need at some point in your life.

STUDENT: I don't see the point of this. They focus a lot more on what you're going to use stuff for in electives than they do in core classes.

TUTOR: Just focus on question number one. I am going to stand right here to make sure you do the work.

STUDENT: I could do this if I just had the opportunity to decide where I was heading within certain academic boundaries, and was given the time and space to reach these goals on my own.

TUTOR: Let's just go step-by-step. Write down the first part of the answer right now while I watch.

STUDENT: I'm not doing this anymore. I can't learn anything with you breathing down my neck.
