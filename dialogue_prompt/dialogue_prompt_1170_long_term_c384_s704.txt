SCENARIO

The student is reading a poem and reviewing blackboard notes about the Holocaust during an introductory lesson to connect with the subject material.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please read the poem 'First They Came for the Jews' and look at the information I jotted down on the blackboard about the Holocaust. Write down your thoughts on the worksheet.

STUDENT: (Looks down at the paper quietly) I felt confused by the poem at first and then we reread it and I understood it a little better.

TUTOR: That's a start. Keep looking at the text and the notes on the board. What do you think about why they were targeted?

STUDENT: (Shrugs slightly) I'm not sure. I know they were hated and segregated in Germany, but it's hard to picture.

TUTOR: Just try to think about the reading and connect to the subject material. What else stands out to you?

STUDENT: I just don't get it. Why didn’t any of the people stick up for themselves?
