SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is meeting with the teacher before the morning bell to get help with a homework assignment.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is almost time for class. Here is the homework assignment.

STUDENT: I have a question about something I do not understand in the homework. I came to your office so you could help me understand the problem.

TUTOR: Just read the instructions again. What do you think the answer is?

STUDENT: I need someone who helps me to do the homework, like those I do not understand. The bell rings at 8:15 a.m. and I want to have the homework ready when I go to class.

TUTOR: Keep trying on your own. I need to finish checking these other papers right now.

STUDENT: (Puts the homework away) I need extra assistance because my parents cannot help me at home due to their language limitations. If you will not provide extra instruction, I will just leave.
