SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Gabriel is arriving at his general education classroom in the morning and is asked to begin his modified reading assignment.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Gabriel, you just arrived at school. Please sit at your desk and start your modified reading assignment.

STUDENT: I don't want to do the reading assignment. I am not doing any school work today.

TUTOR: You need to finish your work, Gabriel. Let's get started on the first page.

STUDENT: No! I was at my mom's house this morning, but now I have to go to my dad's house after school. Everything is changing and I don't like it!

TUTOR: We can talk about that later. Right now, you need to look at your paper and read.

STUDENT: (Pushes the assignment off the desk and starts crying loudly) I am not doing it! I don't know where I am sleeping tonight! I want my old routine back!
