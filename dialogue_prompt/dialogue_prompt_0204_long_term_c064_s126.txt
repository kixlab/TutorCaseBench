SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting with a tutor at the beginning of the semester to plan and organize schoolwork using maths, Swedish, and English textbooks.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Welcome to the beginning of the semester. Here are your maths, Swedish, and English textbooks. We have two semesters ahead of us, so let's look at all the assignments you need to complete.

STUDENT: I remember having a hard time learning things, especially maths, which I still struggle with.

TUTOR: You can go to another room and do the assignments if it helps you concentrate.

STUDENT: Of course I could be in a quiet room but that doesn't provide the right support anyway. I just think of when assignments have to be finished, and how many assignments that have to be finished.

TUTOR: Well, we need to plan out your time management for the whole year. Just try to organize the chapters.

STUDENT: Saying 'now we have two semesters ahead of us' and having a maths textbook, a Swedish textbook, and an English textbook all at the same time makes everything seem endless. I can't organize this, it just creates a feeling of reluctance toward school.
