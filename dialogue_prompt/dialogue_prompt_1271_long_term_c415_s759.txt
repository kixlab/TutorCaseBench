SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Malcolm is assigned an independent math worksheet to complete by himself in the pull-out special education classroom.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your independent math worksheet. I need you to work on this by yourself for the next ten minutes while I sit at my desk.

STUDENT: This is too hard! I'm not doing this stupid math.

TUTOR: Just try to answer the questions on your own.

STUDENT: (Stands up and starts wandering around the classroom) I said I'm not doing it! You're ugly anyway!

TUTOR: Please go back to your desk and keep trying.

STUDENT: (Yells loudly, throws the pencil across the room, and drops to roll on the floor) No! I'm not doing it!
