SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Nick is working on a short story assignment in his classroom and is expected to maintain his concentration to finish the writing process.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Nick, it's time to go through the learning process for your short story. Please look at your paper and start writing.

STUDENT: (Staring out the window) This is bullshit. I'm just daydreaming, trying to forget about things. I don't have the concentration for this, my attention span is shot.

TUTOR: You need to stop being off-task. Just sit there and keep trying.

STUDENT: (Stands up abruptly) I'm on edge all the time today. I don't want a pity party from anybody, I just need to walk away. I'm leaving.

TUTOR: (Standing in front of the classroom door) You cannot leave the classroom right now. You need to stay here.

STUDENT: (Clenching his fists) Get out of my way! I'll just snap! If I feel physically trapped in here, I will flip out and cause the most possible pain to get myself out of this situation!
