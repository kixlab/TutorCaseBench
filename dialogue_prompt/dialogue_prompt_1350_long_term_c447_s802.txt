TUTOR: What do you think about discussing the questions with the rest of the group?

STUDENT: I always appreciate contribution of all members. However, I have to say I feel safer if the contribution comes from a good student. TUTOR: The instructions ask that the group works together to choose the answers.

STUDENT: It's a multiple-choice assessment, and I know that eventually I'm the one who makes the final decisions in choosing the right answers for the group anyway. There's really no need to listen to others; we can just go ahead with using my own answers.
