DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's start on this reading worksheet. I'm going to read the text out loud to you, and then I'll show you how to find the answers.

STUDENT: (Stares away, remaining socially distant and reluctant to participate) I don't need you to read it to me.

TUTOR: It's okay, I'll just do the first one so you can see how it works. The answer to question one is right here in the first sentence.

STUDENT: You should try to let us do the work instead of sitting and watching you do it for us... because that's mostly what the problem is.

TUTOR: I'm just trying to help you get through the assignment. Let's look at question two now.

STUDENT: (Pushes the paper away and completely goes off task) I'm not doing this. We completely go off task because we feel what we're doing is just completely meaningless.
