──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please work on the next three math problems independently at your desk.

STUDENT: (Sits at her desk, looking away from the paper and quietly singing to herself instead of starting the math problems.)

TUTOR: Kylie, please focus on your assignment and try to solve the first problem.

STUDENT: (Quickly writes down a number without showing any work, focused only on the end product, and points to it.) Is this right?

TUTOR: Can you explain to the class how you figured that out?

STUDENT: (Refuses to volunteer her solution or explain her process, looks down, and goes back to playing alone and singing quietly until she is given step-by-step guidance.)
