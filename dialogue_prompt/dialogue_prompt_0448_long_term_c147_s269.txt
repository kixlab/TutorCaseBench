SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is asked to join a group to brainstorm ideas and share personal feelings for their first graffiti mural project.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: For our first graffiti mural, I want you to get into your groups and brainstorm. I'd like you to speak about what you have in your soul and share your inner feelings and emotions with the group to plan the wall.

STUDENT: I'm not working in a group for this. And I'm not talking about what's in my 'soul'. That's weird.

TUTOR: Just try to sit with your group and exteriorize your feelings. What are some things you are angry or passionate about?

STUDENT: I don't have any feelings to show you. I'm always failing at this school stuff anyway, so I don't know why you expect me to have good ideas.

TUTOR: Everyone has something to contribute. Please just join the group and tell them one emotion you want to put on the wall.

STUDENT: (Stands up and pushes chair away) I said I'm not doing it! I'm not sitting in that group talking about my feelings. I'm out of here.
