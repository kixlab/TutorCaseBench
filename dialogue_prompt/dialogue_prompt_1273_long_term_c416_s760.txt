DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is your math worksheet for today. Please complete these problems independently.

STUDENT: (Whining) I don't want to do this math worksheet, it looks too hard.

STUDENT: (Loudly pushes the desk) No! I'm not doing it! I'm not doing any of this work!
