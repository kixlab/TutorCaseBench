SCENARIO

Shirley is attempting to cook a meal from scratch during a cookery course.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Today we are going to cook a meal from scratch. Please go to your station and begin preparing your ingredients.

STUDENT: I have never cooked a meal from scratch before. I am feeling extremely nervous about doing this.

TUTOR: Just look at the recipe and try to get started.

STUDENT: I'm fearful of the other students. Even though we are with our own people who have mental problems and aren't normal people, I'm still terrified of being laughed at if I do something wrong.

TUTOR: Don't worry about the others, just focus on your own cooking and try to get the steps right.

STUDENT: I can't do it. I remember my school days with alarming clarity, and I am so afraid that you are going to ridicule my wrong answers if I make a mistake. I don't know what to do next, but I feel too much embarrassment about asking for help.
