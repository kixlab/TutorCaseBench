SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Patrick is sitting at a table with his peers using crayons and paper to create rubbings of tree bark.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the tree bark, paper, and crayons. Everyone take a spot at the table and start making your bark rubbings.

STUDENT: Hurry up and move, you're in my way!

TUTOR: Let's just try to share the space and use the crayons.

STUDENT: (Kicks the table leg) I don't want to do this with them. They are too slow.

TUTOR: Just put your paper over the bark and rub the crayon on it to see what happens.

STUDENT: I'm bored, bored, bored! I don't want to do rubbings with these crayons!
