DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Now that we are at the final stage of our role-play, who do you think the victim could talk to about the bullying incident?

STUDENT: He can only speak to his family. There is nobody at school he can trust.

TUTOR: What if the victim tries to talk to a teacher?

STUDENT: No. He should not talk to his teacher. If he complains to him, the teacher will not believe him.

TUTOR: Why do you think the teacher wouldn't believe him?

STUDENT: The bully should deny what he did or he could say that the victim had said something bad for him before and then the victim couldn’t prove that this was a lie.
