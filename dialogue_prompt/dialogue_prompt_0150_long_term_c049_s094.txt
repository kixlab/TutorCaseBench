SCENARIO

Ian is in class with his peers and has been assigned a literacy worksheet to complete.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Ian, here is your literacy worksheet for today. Please read the first passage and start answering the questions.

STUDENT: (Leaves the worksheet untouched on the desk, staring at the computer screen instead of starting the assignment)

TUTOR: It has been five minutes, Ian. You need to begin the worksheet now.

STUDENT: Why do I even have to do this? What is the relevance of this primary school reading stuff? I should be working towards getting my driver's licence, not this.

TUTOR: This is the assigned school work for today's lesson. Please just focus and get it done.

STUDENT: (Pushes the worksheet off the desk and starts drumming loudly on the table) I'm not doing it! It's completely irrelevant! (Calls out loudly across the room to his peers) Hey, you guys aren't actually doing this garbage, are you?
