SCENARIO

The student is participating in a whole-class math review where they are expected to raise their hand and wait to be called on.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Class, let's review the math assignment on the board. Who can tell me the answer to the first problem?

STUDENT: (Waves hand frantically in the air and bounces in the seat) Ooh! Teacher! Over here! Look at me!

TUTOR: I am waiting to call on someone who has a quiet hand.

STUDENT: (Sighs loudly, drops hand, and starts poking the student sitting at the next desk with a pencil)

TUTOR: Please remember our classroom rules while we do this activity.

STUDENT: The answer is twenty-four! (Shouts loudly over the other students) I knew it first! I'm not waiting anymore!
