SCENARIO

The student is in a small collaborative group in science class discussing animal welfare after watching a documentary.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Now that we have finished watching the documentary, please discuss what you saw with your group and share your opinions on how the animals were treated.

STUDENT: I was furious watching that movie. They showed dogs that were thrown to a kind of bin, alive and dead.

TUTOR: Please sit down quietly and write your group's answers on the paper.

STUDENT: (Glares at a peer) Don't tell me what to write! If you say something to me I'm going to hit you hard and get crazy!

TUTOR: You need to stop talking like that, sit back in your chair, and get to work right now.

STUDENT: I am not doing anything! (Gets mad and throws his school bag away) I don't respect you and I'm out of control!
