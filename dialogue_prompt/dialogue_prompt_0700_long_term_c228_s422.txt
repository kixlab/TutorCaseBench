──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The class is gathered on the carpet for a read-aloud of 'Separate is Never Equal' to discuss school segregation.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are reading 'Separate is Never Equal'. Let's look at this illustration showing the sign that says 'No dogs or Mexicans allowed'.

STUDENT: (Lifts hands in the air and raises voice) But they are so mean! The white people. I think the white people should go on the other side of the earth!

TUTOR: What do you think about that idea?

STUDENT: That means my brother and me and my mom would be on one side of the earth but my dad would be on the other side. Sad, because then my family would be torn apart like that.

TUTOR: Do you ever notice that people are treated differently because of their skin color?

STUDENT: Well, isn't the book showing us that people do get treated differently because of their skin color? Yeah. I mean, I think that book already answers the question.
