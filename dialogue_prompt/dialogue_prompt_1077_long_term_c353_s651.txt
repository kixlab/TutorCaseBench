STUDENT: (Keeps to himself and looks away from the board) I don't want to sound them out.

TUTOR: Kevin, please sit up and pay attention to the lesson.

STUDENT: (Yells in a loud, angry voice) I don’t want to learn how to read! I don’t even like letters!
