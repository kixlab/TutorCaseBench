SCENARIO

The tutor and Isaac are discussing how to handle negative peer interactions before lunch.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Isaac, let's talk about how you can respond appropriately to your classmates during lunch today.

STUDENT: Why bother? None of my classmates like me and they are always picking on me.

TUTOR: What do you think you should do when you feel like they are picking on you?

STUDENT: I don't know. I usually just play alone anyway. But I can tell you about what happened yesterday that made me so mad.

TUTOR: Just try to ignore them and use your words if they bother you.

STUDENT: (Screams loudly) Words don't work! If they come near me at lunch I'm just going to shove them and start a fight!
