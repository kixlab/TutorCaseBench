TUTOR: You just need to put some effort into it. Keep trying and the words will come to you.

STUDENT: (Pushes the blank assignment away) I’ve never passed an English class in my life. And don’t plan on it now. I’m Mexican so how am I really supposed to be good at this stuff? I'm not doing it.
