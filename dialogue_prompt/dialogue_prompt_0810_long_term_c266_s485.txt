SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

James is sitting at his desk in a cooperative small group, working on a word-making lesson by matching index cards with printed words.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: James, show me how to do the first part of the word-making task with these index cards.

STUDENT: (Sits up in his seat, picks up an index card with the word 'out' printed on it, taps the card softly on the desk, and looks at the other cards on the table for about 4 seconds.)

TUTOR: Put that one next to the card that says 'side'. Now do another one.

STUDENT: (Complies and correctly matches the words 'base' and 'ball'.)

TUTOR: Good job. I am going to walk over to another small group now. Keep working with your group.

STUDENT: (Slouches down in his seat and starts rolling a pencil back and forth across his desk. He watches the other children as they encourage each other to find word matches.)
