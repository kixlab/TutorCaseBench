SCENARIO

The student is playing with plastic tracks and a motor car at the end of a 60-minute session and needs to transition out of the room.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Our 60 minutes are up. It is time to leave the session, so please put the plastic tracks and the motor car away.

STUDENT: (Stops moving the car and stays back, watching instead of packing up. He goes into a shell and doesn't speak.)

TUTOR: What are you doing? You need to pack up your things so we can transition out of the room.

STUDENT: (Avoids eye contact and looks down at his shaking legs.) I'm not feeling well.

TUTOR: We just need to go now. Please stand up and leave the toys on the table.

STUDENT: (Starts crying and becomes completely quiet and withdrawn, refusing to move.) Whenever it's time to leave I want to cry.
