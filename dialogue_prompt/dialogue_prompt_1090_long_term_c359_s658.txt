──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Jasmine is sitting at a round table with a peer and a paraprofessional to complete a math worksheet where she needs to color objects in groups of four.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to complete your math worksheet. Please color the objects on the page in groups of four.

STUDENT: Let’s do some colors! What color is the apple? What color is the boy? What color is the car?

TUTOR: Jasmine, please look at your own paper. You need to do your math.

STUDENT: (Excitedly shakes her braids and rubs her fingers together quickly in front of the ABC book) O the O says/ o/, octopus. the T says…

TUTOR: (Points to the math worksheet) You cannot read the ABC book right now. We have music next, so you need to do your math.

STUDENT: (Grabs the ABC book again) No! Aaahh!
