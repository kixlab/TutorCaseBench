SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is in their fourth-period eighth-grade general education math class, expected to complete practice problems in a peer-supported group.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to complete the practice problems with your group. Please use your calculator to solve the first one.

STUDENT: (Does not follow the direction within 10 seconds and begins doodling instead of writing down answers to the problems.)

TUTOR: You need to work on the math. What do you think the answer is?

STUDENT: I'm not doing it. (Pulls out a non-math book and starts reading.)

TUTOR: Please put the book away and try to do the assignment.

STUDENT: (Verbally refuses to do the work again, puts head down on the desk, and fails to complete the assignment during the allotted time.)
