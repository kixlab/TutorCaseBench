SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is working on an academic task in a mainstream classroom and is expected to present their work at the front of the class.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please finish the rest of the worksheet. When you are finished, I need you to come up to the front of the class to show your example.

STUDENT: I don't know how to do this new part. I'm just going to stop.

TUTOR: Just keep trying. The first question you finished was well done.

STUDENT: No it wasn't well done. It's probably all wrong.

TUTOR: It's time to share now. Please bring your paper to the front of the class so everyone can see.

STUDENT: (Shrinks down in his seat) I'm not going up there. I'm not showing my example to the class, everyone is going to look at me and know I did it wrong.
