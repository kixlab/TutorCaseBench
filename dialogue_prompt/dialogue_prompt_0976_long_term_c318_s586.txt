DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (Shuffles papers from side to side) My backpack is a mess. It's just bits of paper in whirlwind in here.

TUTOR: Okay, let's just find some binder paper so we can start writing the first assignment.

STUDENT: (Ignores the inch of paper in the open binder right in front of him and stands up) I'm going to my room to get some. The family cat is being fairly annoying anyway.
