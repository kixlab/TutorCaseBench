DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please take out your 15-page research paper draft and begin the revision process.

STUDENT: I guess I always thought revision was about fixing spelling.

TUTOR: Look closer at the content. What else do you think needs to be changed?

TUTOR: Keep working on it. Try to rewrite the sections that need improvement.

STUDENT: This is just headwork. Homework was easy. You just had to do it. But revising is like giving yourself the worst headache you've ever had in your life. It's like squeezing out your brain cells on paper.
