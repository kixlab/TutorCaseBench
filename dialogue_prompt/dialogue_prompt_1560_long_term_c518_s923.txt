SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Joseph is expected to sit at his desk and participate in a reading activity while his classmates are present in the room.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Joseph, let's get started on our reading activity. Please sit at your desk and look at the book.

STUDENT: (Squeezes his fidget toy tightly, looking around the classroom and covering his ears) Too loud... I can't.

TUTOR: Just try your best to focus on the reading activity with the rest of the class.

STUDENT: (Throws the fidget toy on the floor, beginning to yell as his frustration grows) No! Too much!

TUTOR: Joseph, please lower your voice and stay in your seat.

STUDENT: (Screams loudly, pushes away from the desk, and runs toward the classroom door to escape, urinating on the floor as he tries to leave the room)
