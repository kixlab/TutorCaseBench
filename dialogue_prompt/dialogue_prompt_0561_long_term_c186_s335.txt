The student is playing with Lego and other toys in the classroom during play time and is asked to put the toys away.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Play time is over. It is time to start putting the toys away.

STUDENT: (Ignores the instruction) Look, I am making a tunnel with Lego!

TUTOR: You need to stop playing and clean up.

STUDENT: (Grabs other objects without permission) I want to play with these! (Messes the girls' houses up)

TUTOR: Please come back here and put the toys in the bin.

STUDENT: (Starts running around the classroom, making a lot of noise and whining) No! I don't want to!
