──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting on the floor by a coffee table, drawing a picture of his family.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the paper on the coffee table. You can start drawing the picture of you and your family now.

STUDENT: But I am not going to sit on the chair, I am going to sit on the floor. (Bends over the coffee table and starts drawing, then begins erasing and redrawing the same spot over and over) The fences of the animal shelters are not good enough.

TUTOR: It looks fine. We need to finish drawing soon.

STUDENT: (Continues etching out minute details on the paper) I am not finished yet.

TUTOR: You have to finish drawing now so we can move on.

STUDENT: (Raises head in a burst of anger) My time! My time lost in vain! (Starts shouting inside the classroom, throws his prize chocolates, and then sits on the floor crying, having a fit)
