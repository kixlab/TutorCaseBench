SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is working in a small group to read cartoon captions and create an original cartoon about bullying on a worksheet.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at the worksheets with the partly-made cartoons. Please read the talking bubbles and draw your own original cartoon.

STUDENT: (Waves hand high) Oh! Oh! I want to talk more about when that kid was mean to me! I was so mad!

TUTOR: Please focus on your worksheet right now.

STUDENT: I don't understand what this phrase in the bubble means. I'm just going to use the half-completed example cartoon instead of making a new one.

TUTOR: Just try your best to think of a new idea.

STUDENT: I don't have any ideas! I would rather discuss the issues than make cartoons. It's too hard to control my emotions when I think about bullying by myself!
