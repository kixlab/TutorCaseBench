──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Joshua is participating in free play activities and is expected to share toy trucks with a classmate.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Joshua, it is time for free play. You and your classmate can play with the toy trucks together.

STUDENT: I am the boss of the trucks. You have to be the bad guy and I get the big truck.

TUTOR: Let's try to share the toys and play nicely.

STUDENT: (Growls at his classmate) No! We have to play my way!

TUTOR: Joshua, please use your words. What do you think is a good way to play together?

STUDENT: (Yells and throws a toy truck) Do this or I will hurt you!
