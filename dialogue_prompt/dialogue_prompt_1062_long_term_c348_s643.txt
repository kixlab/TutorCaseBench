──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is at home trying to answer textbook questions after reading a chapter to prepare for an upcoming test.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please read the chapter and answer the textbook questions to prepare for your test.

STUDENT: I finished reading, but when I try to answer the questions, I find I can't answer them. If I am interested in the material, then I will learn it at once, but if I am not interested in it, then it will take incredibly long to learn it.

TUTOR: Just look back in the text to find the information you missed.

STUDENT: I am looking back in the text, but it's not helping me understand. I want to do this together with someone who knows much about it. And who is prepared to work hard.

TUTOR: Try to write down the answers yourself first before we do anything else.

STUDENT: I can't just write them myself. I need you to sit reading in the book aloud to me. Then we talk about the topic afterwards, or you sit taking notes while I tell you about what I have learned.
