SCENARIO

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We have thirty minutes of writing time today. The prompt is: Everyone has a favorite holiday. Think about your favorite holiday and explain how you celebrate it. Please work independently on your first draft.

STUDENT: (Raises hand immediately) I don't know what to do. I can't get started on my writing.

TUTOR: Keep trying. Look at your paper and add some more details about painting the eggs.

STUDENT: (Raises hand again, complaining) I added 'Once I painted A egg orange and blue whitch is the gator colors', but I can't move further! This is too hard and I need you to come help me right now.
