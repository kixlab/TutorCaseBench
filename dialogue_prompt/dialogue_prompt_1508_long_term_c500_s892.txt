SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Kaylie is participating in a 5-minute conversational interaction session with a peer without disabilities using a Braille communication book.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Kaylie, it is time for your 5-minute conversation session with your peer partner. Please use your Braille communication book to start.

STUDENT: (Rubs her eyes) When I meet new people I’m sort of nervous.

TUTOR: Just try your best to pick a topic from the book.

STUDENT: (Speaks in a continuous monologue without pausing) I listened to a new audio book yesterday about horses and there are so many different breeds of horses, some are very tall and some are small, and I think the small ones are interesting but the tall ones run faster and I want to learn more about them...

TUTOR: Kaylie, you need to make a decision on which question to ask your partner now.

STUDENT: (Begins rubbing her face and smelling the edge of the communication book) Well, I don’t know what I want! I never have enough time!
