SCENARIO

The student is using straws to represent the number 16 and explain the meaning of its digits during a whole-class demonstration.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the number 16 on the board. Please use your straws to show me this number.

STUDENT: (Carefully counts out the straws) Here are sixteen straws.

TUTOR: Thank you. Now, I am pointing to the '6' in '16'. Show me the straws for this number.

STUDENT: (Separates some straws and holds them up) Six.

TUTOR: Alright. Now I am pointing to the '1' in '16'. What does this numeral mean? Show me with the straws.

STUDENT: (Looks puzzled, then holds up a single straw) One.
