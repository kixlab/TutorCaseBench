DIALOGUE

STUDENT: (Stands up from the desk and reaches over) I'm going to use your red pencil.

TUTOR: You need to sit back down and do your own assignment.

STUDENT: (Starts walking around the classroom, talking aloud) I don't want to write! Hey, look at this pencil I got! (Pokes another student's arm)
