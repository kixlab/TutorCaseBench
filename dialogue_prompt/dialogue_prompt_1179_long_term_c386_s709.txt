──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for independent reading. Please sit at your desk and read your book quietly.

STUDENT: (Turns his body away from the assigned materials and stares at the back of the classroom.)

TUTOR: Martin, you need to look at your book.

STUDENT: (Leans over to the next desk) Hey, what page are you on? Look at this.

TUTOR: Please stop talking and focus on your own reading.

STUDENT: (Gets out of his seat and walks away from his desk, leaving his book behind to avoid the reading task.)
