SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

A student is observing their partner during a badminton match and is supposed to transcribe point locations onto a detailed observation worksheet.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the detailed observation worksheet. While your partner plays this set, I need you to watch and record the zones where the points are scored.

STUDENT: I don't see the point of the work required. I'm playing against him next anyway.

TUTOR: Just try your best to observe. What are you going to tell him during the break between sets?

STUDENT: I'll just tell him 'You played well' or 'You played badly'. I don't need to write anything down.

TUTOR: Please look at the paper and try to identify the specific zones.

STUDENT: (Pushes the paper away) No, I'm not filling out the worksheet correctly or identifying the zones. I don't want this.
