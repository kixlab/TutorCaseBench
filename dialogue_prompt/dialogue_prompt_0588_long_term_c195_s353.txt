SCENARIO

The student is working on a math worksheet at their desk while receiving one-on-one instruction from a teacher in a co-taught mainstream classroom.

DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here is the math worksheet for today. Let's go through the first few problems together at your desk.

STUDENT: (Looks at the worksheet and starts writing the answers, keeping their head down and working alone.)

STUDENT: (Continues to look only at the paper, showing no interaction behaviour with the peers nearby.)

STUDENT: (Turns away from the peers to face the teacher completely, continuing to work alone and avoiding any interaction with the rest of the class.)
