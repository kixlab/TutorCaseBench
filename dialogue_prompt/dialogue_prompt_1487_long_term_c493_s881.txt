SCENARIO

The student is sitting at a separated desk in a freshman biology classroom, expected to copy notes from the board.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please sit at your separate desks and copy the biology notes from the board.

STUDENT: (Sits quietly without interacting with anyone) Okay.

TUTOR: You need to participate and get these notes done. Do you have any questions about the material?

STUDENT: No. We don't talk because we get separated, and if we have questions we just ask the teacher.

TUTOR: Well, it is important to write them down. Keep working.

STUDENT: (Puts pencil down) I'm just taking notes that I wouldn't read anyway. Can I just come to you after class to ask about making up the assignment?
