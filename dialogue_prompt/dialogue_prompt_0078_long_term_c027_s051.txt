SCENARIO

The student is in an activity room with access to moderately preferred toys while the therapist sits nearby reading a magazine.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Here are some toys for you to play with. I am going to sit over here and read my magazine.

STUDENT: (The student ignores the toys and begins scratching their arms, digging their nails into the skin.)

TUTOR: Please play with the toys.

STUDENT: (The student continues scratching and begins hitting their torso and legs with their fists.)

TUTOR: I am still reading my magazine, you can keep playing.

STUDENT: (The student escalates to hitting themselves in the head and banging their head against the surface of the table.)
