SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Dennis is sitting in the cafeteria at the beginning of lunch time while his preferred food item is placed on a counter in sight but out of his reach.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Dennis, it is the beginning of lunch time. I am taking your snack and putting it up here on the counter where you can see it.

STUDENT: (Points to the counter) My snack.

TUTOR: Not right now, we are waiting.

STUDENT: (Reaches toward the food) Want it.

TUTOR: You need to sit quietly first.

STUDENT: No! Stop it!
