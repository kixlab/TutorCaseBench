──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Dennis is participating in a Danish lesson where the class is using a camera with a cardboard cover.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Dennis, you took the cardboard cover off the camera. The instruction was to leave it on.

STUDENT: I do bad things. I am noisy.

TUTOR: Since you didn't follow the instructions, you need to go sit in the chair in the corner by the blackboard.

STUDENT: In school they keep an eye on me all the time, so that I do not do stupid things. All the time, I am afraid of doing stupid things and being yelled at.

TUTOR: Please just sit in the corner and face away from the class.

STUDENT: (Accepts being put in the corner in a chair at the blackboard, head faced away from class) I didn't mind. Then I do not have to be afraid of doing stupid things.
