SCENARIO

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at your Knowledge Connections Analyzer data to answer our question: Are we a community that collaborates? What do your reading and build-on percentages show?

STUDENT: For example, reading others' notes, I have read many notes, the percentage is 99% which is pretty high. However, when I look at built-on to others' notes, the percentage is only about 20%, which is pretty low.

TUTOR: What does that data tell you about your interactions?

STUDENT: The data tells me that the interaction between the other students and me is not sufficient. I need to communicate more with peers.

TUTOR: Why do you think your build-on percentage is at 20% right now?

STUDENT: I just look at the quantitative data. I even write notes at home, because I am afraid that my point or idea is not good enough...

Question
