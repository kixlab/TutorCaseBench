SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is in a mainstream classroom attempting to catch up on lost worksheet assignments while peers are present.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please sit at your desk and start catching up on the worksheet you missed yesterday.
