You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Angus is having a one-to-one conversation with his teacher to recount an incident that happened in the playground.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Angus, tell me what happened in the playground.

STUDENT: (Speaking very quickly and getting over-excited) They were over there and then... and then he came up and... it was just...

TUTOR: Keep going, what else happened?

STUDENT: I don't... wait. He took it and then... I don't know what I was saying! (Looks highly flustered)

TUTOR: Just try to explain it to me.

STUDENT: I can't! (Gets visibly annoyed, kicks the chair leg, and turns away, refusing to continue the conversation)

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

C: Use arms to block out the space, creating a visual of what the playground incident looked like in three dimensions and showing where the students were.
D: State that participants are not permitted to use technical language or jargon during the one-to-one conversation about the playground incident.
