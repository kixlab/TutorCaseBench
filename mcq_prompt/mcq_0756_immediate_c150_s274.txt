Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting in a circle participating in a philosophical discussion about whether there is life after death.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Welcome to our discussion circle. Today's question is: Is there life after death?

STUDENT: No way. When you're dead, you're just dead.

TUTOR: Someone else just shared that they believe in an afterlife. What do you think about that?

STUDENT: That's just a story people make up! It doesn't make any sense at all.

TUTOR: Let's wait and hear what the person next to you is saying.

STUDENT: (Interrupting loudly without waiting to be called) But they are wrong! You can't prove it! I'm telling you, there's nothing after you die!

Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────
