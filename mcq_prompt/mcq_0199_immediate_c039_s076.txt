──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Dennis and the school pedagogue are in the hallway at 10:00 AM for him to take his prescribed medication.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Dennis, it is 10 o'clock. It is time to take your medicine.

STUDENT: (Refuses to put the pill in his mouth) When I am home with my father, I do not take my medicine. Then I do not have to behave differently and I can relax.

TUTOR: (Sitting bent over him on the floor) You are at school now, so you need to take it. Please put it in your mouth.

STUDENT: (Lying flat on the floor, tense in his whole body, without making contact) I would like to die.

TUTOR: We don't say things like that. Come on, just take the pill.

STUDENT: ...so I don't have to go to school. I want to cut my hand off because it is just in the way anyhow. (Uses a small metal piece from his booklet to scratch his arm)

Question
