SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is sitting at an oval-shaped table participating in a small group discussion about the book George's Secret Key to the Universe.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's start our small group discussion about George's Secret Key to the Universe. Do you all like reading this together? Who would like to volunteer to share their thoughts first?

STUDENT: (Hesitates and shakes her head no) Kind of... But I like this book. (She holds her head up with her hand and does not volunteer to share).

TUTOR: Anna, what do you think about the chapter we just read?

STUDENT: I think they are going to space... but I don't know if I have the right answer.

TUTOR: Keep discussing it with your group. Talk together about what it might mean.

STUDENT: (Lays her head on the table, her frustrations bringing her to tears) But we are just supposed to talk so the other person can get to know the right answer or if they agree or disagree on each other that they have the right answer or not have the right answer.
