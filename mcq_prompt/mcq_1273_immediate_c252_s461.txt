You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student and interventionist are taking turns reading 'Miss Nelson is missing!' together.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's continue reading 'Miss Nelson is missing!'. I will read this sentence: 'The class was rude.' Now it is your turn.

STUDENT: Was rude or that one.

TUTOR: I just read that part. Please look at the next line and read it.

STUDENT: (Accepts the speaking turn but does not attempt the unread text) Was rude.

TUTOR: (Points to the next passage of text) Go ahead and read this next one.

STUDENT: You 's gonna read the next one. I read this one.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Based on the dialogue above, which intervention is most appropriate for this student in this situation?

B: Agree with the student that they are having a rough time with the reading.
C: Give a warning, followed by a 10-minute timeout if the student does not desist.
D: Ask the student, "Why? Why do you think that?" to prompt a justification.
