followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are going to do a bubble lesson today. Look at the bubbles.

STUDENT: (Shows no interest in interacting with the adult and gives a one-word response) Bubbles.

STUDENT: (Takes the highly motivating item from the table to work away on his own agenda) Open.
