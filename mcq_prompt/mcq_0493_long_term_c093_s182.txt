followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Harry is sitting with a tutor who is reading a text aloud while Harry is asked to follow along in his own book.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: I am going to read this passage aloud now. Please follow along with the text in your book.

STUDENT: (Looks down at the page) Okay.

TUTOR: Are you keeping up with where I am reading?

STUDENT: No, I keep losing my place.

TUTOR: Just keep trying to follow the words.

STUDENT: I'm struggling to follow along with the words. Looking at the words is just becoming a distraction and leading to my inability to focus.
