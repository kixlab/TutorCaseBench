You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is in a small group setting trying to solve a math problem about finding the total number of cubes in 7 towers with 3 cubes each.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at this problem. There are 7 towers with 3 cubes in each. What is the total number of cubes?

STUDENT: (Takes a long time to process the directions, looking down at his hands) Twenty-one.

TUTOR: How did you get to 21?

STUDENT: Um, um, I counted to, um, like, I did this, um, 1, 2, 3, then I had it (raises thumb) then I counted 1, 2, 3, and um, when I did it the first time I got messed up so I went to the beginning.

STUDENT: 1, 2, 3, and 1, I kept it in my head, then 1, 2, 3, that was the second time, then 1, 2, 3, and that was the third time. Wait a minute...
