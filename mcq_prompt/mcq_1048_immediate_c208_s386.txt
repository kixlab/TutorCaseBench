You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Jessica is working one-on-one with a volunteer to write an English label for her drawing of a teacher for the classroom bulletin board.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We need to write an English label for your drawing so we can put it on the classroom bulletin board.

STUDENT: (Looks down at her drawing of a female teacher in front of a board, looking reluctant to attempt writing in English) 음…제가 그리고 싶은 그림은요, 제가 선생님이 되어 있는 모습이요. Umm…The picture I want to draw is, myself who became a teacher and teaching my students.

TUTOR: That is a good idea. Please write that down in English for the label.

STUDENT: (Hesitates, holding her pencil but not writing) 저는…나중에 자라서 선생님이 되고 싶다는. 그런 아이디어가 있어요. I want to be a teacher when I grow up. I have an idea like that.

TUTOR: Okay, go ahead and put those English words on the paper now.

STUDENT: (Stares silently at the paper, unable to come up with the words in English to write her own label. She puts the pencil down, leaving the label completely absent)

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

B: Discuss how to write her Korean idea in English, teaching Jessica the sentence form 'I want to' and the grammar needed to write it independently.
C: Terminate the writing demand and remove the drawing and pencil for 10 seconds, pausing the labeling activity temporarily.
D: Give Jessica a warning, followed by a 10-minute timeout from the labeling activity if she does not begin writing.
