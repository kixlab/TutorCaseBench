──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Today we are examining the chemical components of cigarette smoke. Please follow the exact steps on your record sheet to test the black filter and the cigarette ash.

STUDENT: Why do we have to follow this exactly? This is too prescriptive and rigid, and it's not giving us any room to plan or decide how to do the test.

STUDENT: But this involves a larger amount of previous chemical knowledge. It is not easy to understand what we are even looking at.

TUTOR: Keep trying to get through the steps on the record sheet with your group.

STUDENT: I'm not doing this anymore. I don't enjoy this module because it doesn't make us think and it doesn't let us actually participate. We can't even deal with unexpected difficulties because it's too oriented.
