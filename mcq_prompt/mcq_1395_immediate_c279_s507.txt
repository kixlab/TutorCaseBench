SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Sally is recounting the events of her recent class presentation to her tutor.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Sally, can you tell me what happened when you presented to your class today?

STUDENT: (Maintains eye contact) I stood at the front. I held my project. I read it to the class.

TUTOR: What else happened?
