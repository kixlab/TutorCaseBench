──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Angus is having a one-to-one conversation with his teacher to recount an incident that happened in the playground.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Angus, tell me what happened in the playground.

STUDENT: (Speaking very quickly and getting over-excited) They were over there and then... and then he came up and... it was just...

STUDENT: I don't... wait. He took it and then... I don't know what I was saying! (Looks highly flustered)

TUTOR: Just try to explain it to me.

STUDENT: I can't! (Gets visibly annoyed, kicks the chair leg, and turns away, refusing to continue the conversation)

Question
