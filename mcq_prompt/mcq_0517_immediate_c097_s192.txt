SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a whole-class verbal discussion about a stimulus passage while sitting in a circle.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are going to talk about the stimulus passage we just read. Please share your thoughts with the circle.

STUDENT: I think the story is... well, I want to make a point about the main character, but... (looks over at a classmate who is whispering).

TUTOR: What do you want to say about the character?

TUTOR: Just go ahead and tell us why you think he was wrong.

STUDENT: I can't think when they keep trying to distract me! I don't have my support assistant here to help me stay on task. I can't cope with this right now, I'm removing myself from the session.
