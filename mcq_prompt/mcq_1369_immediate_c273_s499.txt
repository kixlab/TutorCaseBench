SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a weekly support group meeting and is asked to use their diary to reflect on a recent conflict.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at your support group diary and reflect on the conflict that happened yesterday. Can you tell me what the gains and losses were from that situation?

STUDENT: Why do I have to do this? It wasn't my mistake. He was deliberately doing things to annoy me.

TUTOR: We still need to talk about your reaction. Why did you get so angry?

STUDENT: I don't know! I don't understand why I behave in the ways that I do. You're always just trying to blame me.

TUTOR: You just need to calm down and try to control your behavior when we discuss these things.

STUDENT: (Loses temper) I can't control my behaviour when I want to! And I definitely can't control my behaviour when under pressure from you!

Question

C: Allow the student to check out a preferred device to read after completing their support group diary reflection.
D: Use collaborative activities (scenarios, card sorts, prioritisation exercises) with no worksheets, asking, 'What would you do differently if you were faced with the same situation again?'
