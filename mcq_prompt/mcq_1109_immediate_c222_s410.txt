SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a peer-review workshop group to critique drafts and revise their own research paper.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Now that you have received critiques on your draft from your peer-review group, please begin the revision process for your research paper.

STUDENT: I guess I always thought revision was about fixing spelling. It's like I don't even know what to call what I was doing all through high school.

TUTOR: Take a look at the comments your peers left on your draft and see what you can do.

STUDENT: I always thought that peer review was a big waste of time because only the teacher’s opinion matters anyway. When you’re drowning, the last person you think to turn to is someone else who doesn’t know how to swim.

TUTOR: Just try your best to make the changes they suggested.

STUDENT: Revising is way harder than I ever thought. It's just headwork. Revising is like giving yourself the worst headache you’ve ever had in your life, like squeezing out your brain cells on paper.
