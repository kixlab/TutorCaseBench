followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is playing in the park with friends in the sand area.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are playing in the park now. Go ahead and play in the sand with your friends.

STUDENT: (Runs over to the sand area and pushes a friend out of the way)

STUDENT: No! (Hits the friend who tries to sit next to him)

Question
