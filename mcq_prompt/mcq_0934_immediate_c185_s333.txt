followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time to play in the classroom. Please remember we are sharing toys with our friends.
