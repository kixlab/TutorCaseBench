SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Derek is working on an independent reading assignment in the classroom.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please go ahead and read this text independently, and let me know when you are finished with the section.

STUDENT: (Slouches in his chair and quickly flips to the end of the pages) I'm done. I finished it.

TUTOR: That was very fast. What was the passage about?

STUDENT: I don't know. My eyes just seem to skip over words as I’m going down the page.

TUTOR: You need to go back to the beginning and try reading it again so you can understand the meaning.

STUDENT: (Crosses his arms) It makes me feel like I do not want to read anymore. This is really boring. I really want to leave.

Question
