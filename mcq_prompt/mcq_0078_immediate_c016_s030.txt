DIALOGUE

TUTOR: Let's start our discussion on the topic of 'name'. Does anybody have to have a name?

STUDENT: Yes, we have to have names. If not, we could not differentiate people and it will be difficult to call people... when we call hey! All who are present will answer...

STUDENT: (Engages in a loud verbal argument with his friend) Shut up! I don't have to sit down! (Walks away from his desk towards the door) I'm getting out of this class.
