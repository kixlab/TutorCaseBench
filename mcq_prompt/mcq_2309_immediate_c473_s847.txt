SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is requesting an accommodation for an assignment in front of their general education history class.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Class, we are going to begin the history assignment now. Grant, do you have a request to make?

STUDENT: (Looking down at his desk instead of facing the teacher) I need to take this test in the resource room.

TUTOR: Okay. Is there anything else you need to tell me?

STUDENT: (Keeping his head down and avoiding eye contact) No, just that I need to go to the resource room.

TUTOR: Please tell me the reason you need this accommodation.

STUDENT: (Turns his body away from the teacher, refusing to make eye contact while the rest of the class is listening) I'm not saying it. Just forget it, I'll take it here.
