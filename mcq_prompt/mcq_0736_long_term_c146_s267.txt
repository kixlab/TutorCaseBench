SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Benjamin is sitting at his desk during independent work time with the goal of writing sentences.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for independent work. Please get out your paper and start writing your sentences.

STUDENT: Did you know that some superheroes can fly faster than an airplane? Whoosh!

TUTOR: You need to leave the cards alone and start writing your sentences now.

STUDENT: I can't, I'm too busy! The green card means go, so the cars are driving super fast! Vroom! I'm not doing this writing stuff!

Question
