Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Desi is sitting at his desk working on independent textbook activities.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Desi, it is time to start your independent textbook activities at your desk.

STUDENT: (Makes a loud buzzing noise with his mouth) I don't want to do this.

TUTOR: Please stop making noise and look at your book.

STUDENT: Stop telling me what to do! You're always bothering me!

TUTOR: You need to finish your assignment right now.

STUDENT: Leave me alone! If you don't go away, I'm going to rip this textbook up!

Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────
