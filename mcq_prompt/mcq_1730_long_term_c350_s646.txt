You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

During a class session in an honors seminar, the tutor asks Joey to publicly share a metaphor describing his research team's progress with his peers.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Joey, please go ahead and publicly share the metaphor you wrote to describe your research team and your progress.

STUDENT: I described my team as an engine. This engine runs really well on three cylinders, but when the other two spark plug wires are attached, the engine acts almost as if it is supercharged.

TUTOR: What does that mean for your group's dynamics?

STUDENT: Up to this point the engine is running really well but there is so much more performance possible when all the spark plug wires contribute spark.

TUTOR: This is an opportunity to encourage your teammates to become more engaged with the project. Do you have anything else to add about the deadlines or participation?

STUDENT: (Looks uncomfortable) No, we are having relative success. I just want to encourage everyone to fully engage in the research process. Everything is fine.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Based on the dialogue above, which intervention is most appropriate for this student in this situation?

C: Have students work in their research teams of five to develop a master list of expectations, then as a class develop a list of five rules on which they all agree.
D: Inform students their journal entries will not be graded for content and that their identities will be protected through pseudonyms, by changing personal characteristics, and by editing comments.
