SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Patrick is working on electronic equipment in the Timeout-class under the intensive supervision of his teacher.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Patrick, it is time for our afternoon activity. Please stay at the table where I can see you while you work on the electronic equipment.

STUDENT: I can fix this, and I can give you support with the other electronic equipment too. I could even sell you my sound-card if you want to buy it.

TUTOR: Thank you, but let's just focus on finishing the current assignment right now.

STUDENT: I'm doing the work, but we need to change the rules about me being kept in here all day. I want to negotiate my schedule so I have more control over what I do.

TUTOR: That is not something we can change. You have to remain in the Timeout-class under supervision.

STUDENT: I'm not accepting that. I already spoke to the local school representative and the police about this. I am also asking the researchers for argumentative support so that you and my parents will be convinced to let me shoot at the shooting club.

Question
