You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Tony is participating in a painting session with staff using paper and a tube of paint.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our painting session. Here is the paper, the brush, and the tube of paint.

STUDENT: (Holds onto the tube of paint.)

TUTOR: (Tries to dislodge his hand from the tube) Please let go of the paint so we can start.

STUDENT: (He holds on tightly) Heh.

TUTOR: (Pushes his hand onto the paper to start painting) Let's just put your hand here and paint on the paper.

STUDENT: (His smile vanishes, his mouth opens wide until he grimaces, his head turns away from the paper, and he cries.)

Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────
