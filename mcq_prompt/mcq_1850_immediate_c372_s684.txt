SCENARIO

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Everyone, please sit quietly in the pews and take out your notebooks. Before we look at the transportable altar, I am going to lecture on the theoretical concepts of dealing with death and the dying person.

STUDENT: Why do we have to sit and take notes again? I thought we came to the church to actually see what the minister does, not just do more regular school stuff like math or language class.
