SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is asked to begin reading a novel during the daily reading time at the start of the autumn term.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is time for our daily reading. Please pick a novel and start reading the first chapter.
