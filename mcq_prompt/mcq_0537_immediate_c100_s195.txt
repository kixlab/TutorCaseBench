SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Angus is participating in a group discussion about the story Laura and Paul where students must raise their hands to contribute.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's start our group discussion about the story Laura and Paul. Remember to raise your hand if you wish to contribute.

STUDENT: (Looks flustered) But... no! Wait, I lost what I was going to say. She's wrong!
