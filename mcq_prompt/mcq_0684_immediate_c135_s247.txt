SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a small group discussion to learn new vocabulary words from the book 'The Day the Crayons Quit'.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are going to talk about the vocabulary words from 'The Day the Crayons Quit'. The first word is 'declared'.

STUDENT: I know what that means! I want to say it first before anyone else!

TUTOR: What do you think it means?

STUDENT: Declared. It means you say something really loud!

TUTOR: Let's see if your classmates have anything to add.

STUDENT: (Yelling out spontaneously) No, listen to me! I have more to say about it than them!

Question
