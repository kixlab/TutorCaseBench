SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a structured argumentative dialogue circle where they must state if they agree or disagree with previous speakers.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's continue our discussion. Remember to use the format 'I agree or disagree with [person's name] because...'. What do you think about what David just said?

STUDENT: I disagree with David because... it's not fair to take things without asking.

TUTOR: Alright. Now Sarah just said she disagrees with you, because sometimes people have no choice. What is your response to Sarah?

STUDENT: (Frowns) Why is she criticizing me? I guess my viewpoint is just wrong then.

TUTOR: What do you think about her point? Go ahead and answer.

STUDENT: (Looks down at the floor and stays completely silent, refusing to contribute any further to the dialogue)

Question
