──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please solve this arithmetic exercise on the paper: 2/3 * 9.

STUDENT: (Looks at paper) Now, here I'll put two thirds times nine and I'll make from it one thing so it will be easier for me... So, here I'll write... I'll do like this, two times nine divided by three times one. (Looks at interviewer) Is it right to do like that?

TUTOR: What do you think you should do next?

STUDENT: (Quietly, biting lip, looking down) With fractions I'm not good so I don't know... (Looks up at interviewer, raised, contracted eyebrows, slightly anxious expression) Maybe by half?

TUTOR: Just keep trying to solve it.

STUDENT: (Thinks) It can be simplified. (Erases what she has written) Because I have to solve the exercise from the beginning... I simplify the nine and then I'll get three and one.
