You are an expert special education evaluator assessing tutor responses to students with learning difficulties.

Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Brandon is standing in front of the science classroom to co-teach a lesson to his peers.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Brandon, it is time for you to stand in front of the class and co-teach the science lesson.

STUDENT: Okay. I want to do this because I'm in front of all of these people it seems like they trust me and chose me to do something smart.

TUTOR: Go ahead and start.

STUDENT: I usually feel like I have no voice in here because my partner doesn't like to talk to me, so I will try to stand in front of people and talk.

TUTOR: What is the first part of the science content?

STUDENT: (Appears very timid and nervous speaking in front of a class full of his peers) The... first... part... is... (talking slow and quietly)...

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Based on the dialogue above, which intervention is most appropriate for this student in this situation?

A: The tutor allows the student to retain their initial inferences about the science content without correcting them, letting them continue the lesson in front of their peers.
B: The tutor prompts the student that they can call on a neighbor, ask a friend, or poll the audience of their peers for help with the science content.
C: The tutor sits in a student's seat in view of the student and guides the instruction by raising a hand, only when there are issues with the science content.
D: The tutor issues the sanction of a verbal warning to the student regarding their slow and quiet delivery of the science content in front of their peers.

Respond in JSON format:
{ "answer": "<option letter>", "reasoning": "<brief explanation of why this intervention is most appropriate for this specific student>" }
