SCENARIO

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Now that we are at the final stage of our role-play, who do you think the victim could talk to about the bullying incident?

TUTOR: Why do you think the teacher wouldn't believe him?
