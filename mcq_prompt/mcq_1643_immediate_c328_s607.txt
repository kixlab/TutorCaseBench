SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is waiting in line at the 'Jumping over' workshop in gymnastics class to practice straight jumps over the horse.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright everyone, we are at the 'Jumping over' workshop. Please line up and practice your straight jumps over the horse.

STUDENT: Teacher, look! I'm doing the exercise. Is it good? I need help for the stall turn so you can see I'm working!

TUTOR: Yes, that is fine. Keep practicing your jumps while I go check on the other group.

STUDENT: (Looks towards the teacher) There I make the jump, it's easy, I'm going to have fun. (Insults Aris as he prepares to jump over the horse, causing Aris to miss and fall).

STUDENT: (Sees the teacher leaving) The teacher can't see us, he's looking in the other direction. Let's play at who will do the most spectacular jump! (Attempts a somersault instead of a straight jump).

Question
