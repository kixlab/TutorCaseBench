──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Jay is sitting at his desk in his third-grade classroom as the teacher directs the class to begin their independent math practice worksheet.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Alright everyone, we've finished going over the new math concepts. It's time to begin your independent practice on the worksheet at your desks.

STUDENT: (Fails to attempt his individual seatwork and blurts out loudly) This is too hard! I'm not doing this!

TUTOR: Jay, please focus on your own paper and try to start the first problem.

STUDENT: (Bellows across the room) I said I don't know how to do it! It's impossible!

TUTOR: Just do your best and keep trying.

STUDENT: (Persistently calls out and bellows, causing disruptions to the point where other students complain, while still failing to attempt the seatwork) I need help! You have to show me! I'm not doing it!

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────

D: Conduct scaffolding conversations in chunks over the course of the math lesson, coming and going from his desk, then resuming the next part of the conversation.
