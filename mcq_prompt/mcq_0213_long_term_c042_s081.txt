SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a classroom discussion about the character traits and vulnerabilities of Achilles in literature.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at the literature about Achilles and discuss his character traits and vulnerabilities.

STUDENT: Actually, the concept of a physical vulnerability like that is scientifically inaccurate, and I can defend my position on why the text is wrong.

TUTOR: We need to focus on the story as it is written, please stop arguing.

STUDENT: (Looking anxious) I'm just defending my position. Why are you getting mad at me?

STUDENT: It isn't my fault that I'm in trouble! It's my ADHD. My brain is wired to argue and focus on these details, so you can't hold me responsible for not doing the assignment the way you want. It's a medical diagnosis, so technically I shouldn't be in trouble.
