SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student and teacher are quietly conferencing to review the student's reading log and assess their personal reading goals.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's look at your reading log and reflect on your effort this week. How would you describe yourself as a reader right now?

STUDENT: I'd say I'm pretty good at reading. I read pretty slowly and think about what is happening instead of just reading through it and going back and thinking about it.

TUTOR: You've finished quite a few self-selected books lately. What does that tell you?

STUDENT: I mean, I'm a reader, kind of. But it takes me a long time to complete a title, unless I find a book I really like.

TUTOR: How should we write down your reading goals and evaluate your overall success for this conference?

STUDENT: Just put that I’m not really all that good in reading. I take a Reading 180 class ‘cause I read really slow. Even though I like reading for fun, I have ADHD and struggle to answer comprehension questions. It takes me awhile to read 50 pages; I’m a slow reader.
