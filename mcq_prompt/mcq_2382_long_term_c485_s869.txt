SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Tyrone is sitting in the general classroom working on revising his draft to add creativity and interesting language.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Tyrone, it is time to revise your draft. Please add some creativity, interesting language, and feeling to your writing.

STUDENT: (Sits quietly without being able to start writing. He looks around, finding it difficult to concentrate in the noisy atmosphere of the classroom.)

STUDENT: (He has trouble generating and articulating ideas. He slowly writes on his paper: "I would read a book . play vido games with my brother .")
