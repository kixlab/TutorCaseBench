followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is playing with toys alongside peers in a large mainstream classroom during break time.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: It is playtime in the big classroom, so go ahead and play with the toys with the other children.

STUDENT: I don’t like it when we don’t play.

STUDENT: The classroom has more toys and more colours, but I want more well-behaved peers.

TUTOR: Why do you say that?
