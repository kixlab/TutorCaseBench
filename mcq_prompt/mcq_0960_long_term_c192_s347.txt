Below is a tutoring dialogue between a tutor and a student with learning difficulties,
followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student is participating in a journal writing activity to reflect on the upcoming move to a new school.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's work on our feeling journals today. Please draw or write about the upcoming move to the new school.

STUDENT: (Looking at the pictures of the current preschool) I feel a little nervous. This is where we did this project.

TUTOR: Just write down what you are thinking about.

STUDENT: I will miss the playground because I would play here.

TUTOR: Keep drawing in your journal.

STUDENT: (Pushes the journal away) I don’t want to move. I like this school.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────
