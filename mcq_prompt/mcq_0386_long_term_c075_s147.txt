SCENARIO

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's play our guessing game. I am thinking of someone. His family is from Cameroon, he has a twin brother, and he likes math materials. Is it Bernie?

STUDENT: (Grins enormously, nods, and bends and straightens twice in a row.)

TUTOR: Is your family from Cameroon, Bernie? What part of the world is that in?

STUDENT: (Stands rigidly and does not answer; he is no longer smiling. Looks up and then down to his feet.) I have a rocket at home.
