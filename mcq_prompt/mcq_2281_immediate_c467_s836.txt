SCENARIO

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Let's review the survey you are revising on the laptop to print out and distribute to your fellow students.

STUDENT: (Moving desks closer together) I feel a lot of people will answer ‘yes’ to this one. You know why? Cause I have a LOT of friends like that.

TUTOR: How are you planning to handle the completed surveys?
