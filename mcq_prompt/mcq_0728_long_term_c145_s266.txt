SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Craig is supposed to be completing a reading assignment during independent work time in a general classroom setting with cluster seating.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: Please start your independent work on the reading assignment. We will be working quietly for the next fifteen minutes.

STUDENT: (Turns to a peer at his cluster) Did you guys watch that new movie that came out yesterday?

STUDENT: Okay. (Pauses, then leans over to another peer) Are you going to the park after school?

STUDENT: (Laughs as a peer responds back) I know, right? Hey, what level did you get to on that game last night?
