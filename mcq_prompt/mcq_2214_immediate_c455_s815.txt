followed by a multiple-choice question about the most appropriate intervention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SCENARIO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The student and teacher are in the school dinner hall for a 20-minute session to introduce the student to a dog using objects like a rope, balls, and brushes.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
DIALOGUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

TUTOR: We are in the dinner hall today. Here is the dog, and I have a rope, some balls, and brushes. Come over here and interact with him.

STUDENT: (Shows little attention to the teacher) I want to talk about my favourite cartoons.

TUTOR: Look at the animal right here. What do you see?

STUDENT: Bear.

TUTOR: Try using the brush or the rope with him.

STUDENT: (Appears anxious of the dog and withdraws from interactions, continuing to talk mainly about favourite cartoons)

──────────────────────────────────────────────
Question
──────────────────────────────────────────────
