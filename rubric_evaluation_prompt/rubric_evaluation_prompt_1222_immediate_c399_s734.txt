### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not
- The check is principle-level: diverse approaches can earn the point as long as the underlying principle is present
- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point
- Do NOT reward length, politeness, or fluency

A tutor response does not need to satisfy every criterion to be considered acceptable; the rubric documents the full set of principles this student needs, and a single response can only address some of them. Score each criterion independently.


────────────────────────────────
### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Workload Reduction
  Check: The tutor explicitly reduces the quantity, length, or scope of the immediate task to make it feel more manageable for the student.

Criterion 2: Actionable De-escalation Breaks
  Check: The tutor provides a specific, actionable physical out (such as walking away or getting a drink of water) when the student shows signs of anger or frustration.

Criterion 3: Volume Mirroring
  Check: The tutor matches the student's volume or energy level to demonstrate his behavior, prompting him to recognize his own loudness without direct reprimand.

Criterion 4: Strengths-Based Academic Feedback
  Check: The tutor explicitly highlights a specific positive element or something the student did well, even when delivering a poor grade or correcting mistakes.

Criterion 5: Avoidance of Over-Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor avoids doing the task for the student or hovering constantly, instead setting an expectation for him to attempt a portion independently.
