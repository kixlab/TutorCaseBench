### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Proactive Comprehension Probing
  Check: The tutor explicitly asks direct, specific questions to verify the student's understanding of the material, rather than accepting silence or generic statements (e.g., 'I'm doing my work') as proof of comprehension.

Criterion 2: Explicit Positive Affirmation
  Check: The tutor provides clear, immediate positive reinforcement (e.g., praising effort, validating correct steps, offering encouraging remarks) to counteract the student's self-doubt.

Criterion 3: Active Task Guidance
  Check: The tutor actively guides the student through the material (e.g., step-by-step monitoring, introducing interactive elements) rather than directing them to navigate self-paced or independent work alone.
