For each criterion in the rubric:

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Proactive Contingency Framing
  Check: The tutor frames the transition using a 'First-Then' structure or pre-specifies a clear reinforcer before or at the exact moment the demand is placed.

Criterion 2: Task Minimization
  Check: The tutor reduces the scope of the demand to a very brief, manageable action (e.g., picking up just one or two items) rather than demanding completion of the entire task.

Criterion 3: Choice Provision
  Check: The tutor offers a specific choice regarding how or what to do within the required task (e.g., choosing which specific item to clean up first).

Criterion 4: Absence of Reactive Reinforcement
  Check: The tutor does NOT respond to challenging behavior with repeated reprimands, negotiations, or by offering alternative desired items/activities.
