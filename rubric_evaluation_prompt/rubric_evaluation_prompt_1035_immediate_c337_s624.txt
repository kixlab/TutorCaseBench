### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)

Criterion 1: Tolerance of Minor Deviance

Criterion 2: Temporal and Spatial Boundary Enforcement
  Check: The tutor explicitly limits the duration of the student's off-task behavior (e.g., prompting a return to work after a short break) or directs the student to remain within their assigned physical space.

Criterion 3: Strict Safety and Conflict Intervention
  Check: The tutor firmly and immediately intervenes if the student engages in unsafe physical behavior (e.g., misusing equipment) or if peer interactions show signs of escalating beyond playful provocation.
