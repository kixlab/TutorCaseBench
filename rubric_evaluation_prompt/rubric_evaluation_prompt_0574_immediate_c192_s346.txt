### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Direct Confirmation of Consistency
  Check: The tutor provides explicit, concrete reassurance answering the student's specific questions about what will remain the same (e.g., teachers, toys, playground), rather than deflecting or asking open-ended questions.

Criterion 2: Tangible Material Selection
  Check: The tutor offers a concrete, participatory method for the student to identify, label, or secure specific items to bring to the new environment (e.g., making a list, using sticky notes, drawing the items).

Criterion 3: Facilitated Emotional Expression
  Check: The tutor explicitly acknowledges the student's expressed emotion and guides them to explore it through a concrete outlet, such as a feeling journal, drawing, or a focused validating discussion.
