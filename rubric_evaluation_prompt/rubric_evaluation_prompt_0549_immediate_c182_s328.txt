For each criterion in the rubric:

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Advance Transition Warnings
  Check: The tutor provides a clear warning, countdown, or heads-up before a transition or new activity begins.

Criterion 2: Emotional and Expectation Triage
  Check: The tutor asks a question to gauge the student's emotional state or explicitly reminds them of a specific behavioral expectation before or during a task.

Criterion 3: Supported Follow-Through
  Check: The tutor offers direct assistance, guided help, or a collaborative approach to complete the requested task when the student resists.

Criterion 4: Planned Ignoring of Tantrums
  Check: The tutor deliberately ignores tantrum behaviors (e.g., yelling, throwing) by redirecting their own focus, stepping back, or withholding verbal attention from the negative behavior.

Criterion 5: Concrete 'If-Then' or Visual Referencing
  Check: The tutor explicitly references a visual support (e.g., schedule, picture, cue card) or uses a clear 'If [action], then [result]' statement.
