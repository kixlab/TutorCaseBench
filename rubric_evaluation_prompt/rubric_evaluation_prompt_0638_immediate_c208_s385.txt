### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Low-Pressure Home Language Comprehension Checks
  Check: The tutor uses the student's home language (Korean) to gently ask if she understands the instruction or content, rather than relying on her non-verbal cues or asking in English.

Criterion 2: Dual-Language Content Delivery
  Check: The tutor presents instructional material by alternating between English and the student's home language, providing home-language explanations for the English text or concepts.

Criterion 3: Explicit Emotional Validation
  Check: The tutor explicitly validates the student's ideas or efforts using encouraging, low-stakes phrases (e.g., 'any idea will do', 'that is a good idea') to reduce her anxiety.

Criterion 4: Idea-to-Frame Translanguaging
  Check: The tutor allows the student to express her idea in her home language first, and then provides a specific English sentence frame or grammatical structure to help her translate that exact idea into English.
