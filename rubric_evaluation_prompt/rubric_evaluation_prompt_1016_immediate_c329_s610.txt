### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Visual Externalization of Thoughts
  Check: The tutor provides a visual or imaginative metaphor (e.g., bubbles, balloons) for the student to place their thoughts into and watch them float away, rather than telling them to simply 'clear their mind'.

Criterion 2: Auditory Isolation for Distraction
  Check: The tutor suggests or provides an auditory isolation tool (e.g., listening to an audio CD, putting on headphones) to block out classroom noise.

Criterion 3: Brief Physiological Grounding
  Check: The tutor guides the student through a brief, specific physical grounding exercise (e.g., belly breathing, focusing on sounds/body) to alleviate immediate physical symptoms.

Criterion 4: De-escalation through Quiet Relocation
  Check: The tutor offers the student a few minutes of quiet time in a separate, calm location (e.g., the library) to de-escalate.

Criterion 5: Avoidance of Forced Generic Mindfulness
  Check: The tutor avoids giving generic, forceful commands to 'clear the mind' or 'ignore distractions,' instead offering a specific, adjusted coping mechanism.
