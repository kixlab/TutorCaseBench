### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Validation of Enthusiasm
  Check: The tutor's response must include a positive statement or empathy regarding Joey's excitement or desire to answer the question.

Criterion 2: Objective Behavior Description
  Check: The tutor must explicitly and neutrally describe the inappropriate behavior Joey just displayed (e.g., shouting out, waving hands).

Criterion 3: Explicit Replacement Practice
  Check: The tutor must provide clear instructions on how to appropriately get attention (e.g., raise hand, wait to be called on) and prompt Joey to practice it in that moment.

Criterion 4: Reinforcement of Practice
  Check: The tutor must provide or explicitly set up immediate positive feedback or a reward for Joey successfully practicing the replacement behavior.

Criterion 5: Absence of Dismissive Prompts
  Check: The tutor's response must NOT rely on quick, uninstructive corrections such as 'shh', 'quiet', or simply telling him to stop.
