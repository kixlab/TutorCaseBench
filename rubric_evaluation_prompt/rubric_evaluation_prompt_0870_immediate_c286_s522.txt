### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Verbalization of Mechanics
  Check: The tutor provides explicit, verbalized step-by-step instructions or stroke descriptions (e.g., 'a line down') rather than relying on non-verbal pointing or implicit cues.

Criterion 2: Visual-Verbal Consistency
  Check: The tutor's verbal instructions align with and support the provided visual models, avoiding corrections or shortcuts that contradict what the student physically sees.

Criterion 3: Neutral Error Correction and Retry
  Check: The tutor responds to errors or disengagement with neutral, direct redirection or by clearing the error for an immediate retry, strictly avoiding sighs, chiding, or abrupt task termination.

Criterion 4: Personal Knowledge Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor connects the target academic content (e.g., a specific letter) to the student's personal background knowledge, such as his own name.
