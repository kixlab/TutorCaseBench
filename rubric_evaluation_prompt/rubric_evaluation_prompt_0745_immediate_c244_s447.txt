### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Action-First Engagement
  Check: The tutor's response allows the student to immediately begin playing or trying the activity before engaging in extended discussion or reflection.

  Check: The tutor provides technical instruction or feedback within the context of continuous gameplay, completely avoiding the use of isolated individual or paired drills.

Criterion 3: Novelty for Skill Acquisition
  Check: If introducing a specific rule or principle to learn, the tutor applies it to an unfamiliar sport or activity rather than modifying the rules of a sport the student already knows well.

Criterion 4: Protection from Peer Critique
  Check: The tutor's response does not ask the student to evaluate, assess, or identify the weaknesses of their peers.
