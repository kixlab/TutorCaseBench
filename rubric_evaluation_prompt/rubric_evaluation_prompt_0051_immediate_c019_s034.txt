Criterion 1: Prompting Physical Spatial Blocking

Criterion 3: Dynamic Representation of Extensions
  Check: The tutor describes or asks the student to perform a dynamic physical motion (e.g., sliding a finger outward, pulling an object out) to represent an extension beyond the main 3D shape.

Criterion 4: Avoidance of Purely 2D/Verbal Explanations
  Check: The tutor does NOT rely exclusively on referencing 2D diagram labels or mathematical formulas; the response must incorporate a physical, spatial, or gestural visualization strategy.
