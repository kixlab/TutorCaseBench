### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Low-Barrier Response Mechanisms
  Check: The tutor provides a low-stakes, structured, or non-verbal way for the student to participate (e.g., a rating scale, multiple choice, or visual signal) rather than relying solely on open-ended verbal elaboration.

Criterion 2: Explicit Processing Time
  Check: The tutor explicitly builds in or encourages a pause for thinking (e.g., 'take a moment to think' or 'hands down and think') before expecting a response.

Criterion 3: Visual Anchoring of Ideas
  Check: The tutor uses or suggests using a visual representation (e.g., writing on a board, creating a visual rule, or explicitly noting that the idea will be written down) to anchor the verbal discussion.

Criterion 4: Contribution Formalization
  Check: The tutor explicitly validates the student's brief contribution and rephrases or formalizes it into a broader concept or rule, rather than demanding the student elaborate verbally.
