### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Non-Directive Commentary
  Check: The tutor uses comments, observations, or shared affect (e.g., laughing, praising) to engage the student, explicitly avoiding direct questions or commands.

Criterion 2: Holistic Redirection from Part-Object Fixation
  Check: If the student fixates intensely on a specific physical part of an object or being, the tutor gently intervenes to redirect their attention to the whole entity.

Criterion 3: Triadic Engagement via Shared Stimuli
  Check: The tutor uses the student's focus on the object/animal as a bridge to build joint attention with the student (e.g., by joining the play or suggesting a turn-taking game) rather than just directing the student-animal interaction.

Criterion 4: Arousal and Disengagement Regulation
  Check: When the student shows signs of sudden disengagement or over-arousal, the tutor reduces pressure and facilitates regulation rather than prompting them to return to the task.
