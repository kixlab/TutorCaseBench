### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Enlarged Interaction Targets
  Check: The tutor's long-term environment design ensures that clickable trigger areas are significantly enlarged (e.g., clicking a whole box instead of a single small item) to accommodate imprecise mouse movements.

Criterion 2: Extraneous Pop-up Removal
  Check: The tutor proactively disables or removes unnecessary system information, privilege warnings, and foreign-language pop-ups from the learning interface.

Criterion 3: Strict Task Volume Capping
  Check: The tutor structures all learning sessions and assessments to contain a strict maximum of six items or questions per round.

Criterion 4: Sensory and Movement Regulation
  Check: The tutor configures the virtual environment to disable fast avatar movements (like teleporting or flying) and minimizes background music to a comfortable, non-triggering level.

Criterion 5: Sequential and Gradual Pacing
  Check: The tutor plans a sequential curriculum that introduces basic skills (like naming objects) one at a time, explicitly avoiding advanced reasoning or multi-variable grouping tasks until foundational readiness is shown.
