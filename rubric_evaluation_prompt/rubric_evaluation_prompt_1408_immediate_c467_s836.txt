### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Demystification of Professional Conventions
  Check: The tutor explicitly explains the meaning, purpose, or mechanics of a professional convention (e.g., formal phrasing, email etiquette) without making the student feel inadequate for not already knowing it.

Criterion 2: Validation of Tentative Efforts
  Check: The tutor explicitly validates the student's attempt, mistake, or impulsive action by framing it positively as a step toward progress or a natural part of the learning process.

Criterion 3: Bridging to Lived Experience
  Check: The tutor uses analogies, idioms, or cultural references to connect the academic or professional task to the student's real-world experiences or understanding.

Criterion 4: Collaborative Scaffolding of Revisions
  Check: The tutor prompts a discussion about why certain content (e.g., survey questions, email drafts) might need to be adjusted for the target audience, rather than simply dictating the changes.
