### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Sensory and Energy Accommodation
  Check: The tutor accommodates the student's sensory distractions or need for physical energy regulation (e.g., allowing a physical break, removing the distractor) rather than simply demanding she focus on the task.

Criterion 2: Concrete Task Structuring
  Check: The tutor provides clear, specific, and concrete steps for how to proceed with the immediate task rather than giving vague directives to 'keep working'.

Criterion 3: Error Normalization and De-escalation
  Check: The tutor responds to errors or confusion with neutral, supportive feedback that normalizes the struggle and explicitly avoids shaming or punitive language.

Criterion 4: Safe Engagement and Stress Reduction
  Check: The tutor allows the student to process new material privately or step away from a stressor, explicitly avoiding forcing her into immediate public performance.
