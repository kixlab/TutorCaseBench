### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Real-World Contextualization
  Check: The tutor's response must frame the concept within a concrete, real-world application or physical experiment rather than presenting it solely as an abstract rule or formula.

Criterion 2: Active Co-creation of Knowledge
  Check: The tutor must prompt the student to actively contribute an idea, observation, or step in the problem-solving process, explicitly avoiding instructions to merely watch, listen, or copy.

Criterion 3: Calibrated Exploratory Prompting
  Check: The tutor must provide an exploratory prompt or question that is accessible enough for the student to attempt immediately without feeling overwhelmed or left behind.

Criterion 4: Individual Accountability
  Check: The tutor must direct a specific, manageable question or task directly to this student to prevent them from deferring to peers or hiding in the group.
