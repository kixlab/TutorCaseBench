### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Proactive Barrier Removal
  Check: The tutor's response must proactively offer or provide the missing resource/material without penalizing, shaming, or lecturing the student for not having it.

Criterion 2: Low-Stakes Engagement
  Check: The tutor must direct the student to a simple, low-friction task (e.g., reading a preferred book) or a strategic, low-pressure environment rather than forcing immediate compliance with the standard task.

Criterion 3: Absence of Lecturing
  Check: The tutor's response must completely avoid lecturing, preaching, or reprimanding the student about rules, responsibility, or his defiant attitude.
