### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Proceeding Through Resistance
  Check: The tutor continues with the literacy content or explores the student's underlying thought rather than correcting or dwelling on the student's initial complaints or sighs.

Criterion 2: Validating Spontaneous Contributions
  Check: The tutor accepts and engages with the student's unprompted verbal connections or enthusiasm instead of reprimanding them for talking out of turn.

Criterion 3: Reframing Movement as Processing
  Check: The tutor allows or redirects the student's physical movement or play into the literacy task rather than demanding they sit still and concentrate.

Criterion 4: Facilitating Peer Collaboration
  Check: The tutor encourages the student to collaborate, share turns, or seek help from a peer rather than forcing isolated, independent reading.
