Criterion 1: Require Symbolic Exchange

Criterion 3: Multimodal Instruction Delivery
  Check: The tutor pairs verbal instructions or prompts with gestures, specific tonality, or visual supports.

Criterion 4: Utilize Fixations for Engagement
  Check: The tutor incorporates the student's current focus, fixation, or nonverbal behavior into the activity to initiate a communication exchange, rather than forcing a sudden transition.
