### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Choice-Based Elaboration
  Check: The tutor provides explicit options or choices when asking the student to add details or expand on an idea, and avoids open-ended demands for elaboration (e.g., 'What else?').

Criterion 2: Visual Anchoring
  Check: The tutor explicitly directs the student's attention to a visual aid (e.g., a photo, video, or communication book) to prompt ideas, sequencing, or dialogue.

Criterion 3: Non-Verbal Participation Pathways
  Check: The tutor offers or validates a non-verbal way for the student to contribute to the task (e.g., miming, pointing, taking a picture, or acting out a role).

Criterion 4: Modeling Expressive Language
  Check: The tutor models the desired language by demonstrating vocal expression or using specific adjectives/adverbs before asking the student to try.
