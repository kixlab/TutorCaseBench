### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Immediate Attention Acknowledgment
  Check: The tutor immediately acknowledges the student's bid for attention or help, rather than ignoring it, delaying, or telling them to 'just keep working'.

Criterion 2: High-Frequency Response Opportunities
  Check: The tutor embeds frequent, specific prompts or questions that require the student to actively respond, rather than delivering long monologues.

Criterion 3: Active Instructional Presence
  Check: The tutor actively explains, models, or discusses the material with the student, avoiding periods of silent, unguided independent work.

Criterion 4: Positive Reinforcement and Reframing
  Check: The tutor explicitly praises the student's effort/behavior, or pairs any necessary correction with positive acknowledgment and clear, supportive expectations rather than a standalone reprimand.
