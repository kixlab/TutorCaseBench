### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Active External Prompting
  Check: The tutor provides direct, active prompting or encouragement to keep the student engaged with the current task, rather than waiting for the student to initiate or expecting independent persistence.

Criterion 2: Concrete Calculation Support
  Check: The tutor suggests or encourages the use of physical, concrete aids (e.g., rulers, number lines, or finger counting) to help the student solve the math problem.

Criterion 3: Failure Bypass Strategy
  Check: The tutor explicitly suggests skipping, guessing, or moving on to the next question when the student is stuck, specifically providing an alternative to resetting the task.

Criterion 4: Time-Pressure Avoidance
  Check: The tutor's response is completely free of time limits, countdowns, or references to speed and timers.
