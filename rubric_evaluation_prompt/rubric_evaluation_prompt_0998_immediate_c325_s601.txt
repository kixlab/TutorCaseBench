### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Intense 1:1 Task Proximity
  Check: The tutor offers to work directly side-by-side with the student on the task rather than repeatedly directing him to work independently.

Criterion 2: Respectful Redirection via Validation
  Check: When correcting out-of-turn or disruptive behavior, the tutor explicitly acknowledges the value of the student's ideas or expresses a genuine desire to hear them at the appropriate time.

Criterion 3: Expertise Positioning
  Check: The tutor explicitly invites the student to share their specific knowledge, ideas, or expertise on the topic being discussed.

Criterion 4: Refusal of Power Struggles
  Check: The tutor ignores minor provocations (e.g., silly noises, sassy tone, broken pencils) and avoids back-and-forth arguing over compliance.
