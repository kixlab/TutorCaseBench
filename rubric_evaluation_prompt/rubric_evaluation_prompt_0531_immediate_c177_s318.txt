### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:

- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not

- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Avoidance of Procedural Dictation
  Check: The tutor's response does not dictate step-by-step procedural instructions or explicitly tell the student exactly how to format or execute the math problem.

Criterion 2: Integration of Action and Speed Interests
  Check: The tutor incorporates elements of physical action, speed, or the student's natural interests into the immediate framing of the math task.

Criterion 3: Active Measurement or Recording Role
  Check: The tutor assigns or encourages a specific, active role for the student focused on precise measurement, timing, or recording data.

Criterion 4: Space for Independent Momentum
  Check: The tutor provides space for the student to drive the inquiry, either by stepping back when the student shows confidence or by prompting the student to ask and answer their own questions.
