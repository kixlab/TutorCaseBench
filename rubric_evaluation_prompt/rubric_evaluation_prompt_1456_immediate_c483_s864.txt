### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:

- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not

- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Pre-Instruction Attention Verification
  Check: The tutor ensures the student is visually attending to them or the materials before delivering the instruction or discriminative stimulus.

Criterion 2: Immediate Reinforcement Delivery
  Check: The tutor provides or explicitly promises immediate reinforcement the exact moment the student completes the desired behavior.

Criterion 3: Extinction of Outbursts
  Check: The tutor ignores problem behaviors (crying, hitting, throwing) without offering comfort, verbal negotiation, or the desired object in response to the outburst.

Criterion 4: Contingent Use of Obsession
  Check: The tutor uses the 'Lemon Girl' book strictly as a reinforcer for compliance, ensuring it is removed from her hands before the task begins.

Criterion 5: Inter-Trial Engagement
  Check: The tutor incorporates brief, entertaining activities, songs, or games during the intervals between instructional trials.
