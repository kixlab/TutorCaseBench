### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Framing Behavior as a Choice
  Check: The tutor explicitly points out a behavioral or focus decision the student is making, or asks the student how they can make a better choice regarding their attention.

Criterion 2: Providing Social Lifelines
  Check: The tutor offers the student a specific option to seek help from others (e.g., asking a friend, polling the audience) when they are stuck or unsure.

Criterion 3: Interrupting Repetitive Solitary Tasks
  Check: The tutor avoids forcing the student through prolonged repetitive drills by either shifting the activity or transforming a solitary task into a collaborative or group effort.

Criterion 4: Pacing for Comprehension
  Check: The tutor prompts the student to slow down their reading pace or pauses them specifically to check for meaning, rather than allowing them to speed-read unchecked.
