### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Empathetic Validation of Frustration
  Check: The tutor explicitly acknowledges and validates the student's feelings or frustrations before attempting to redirect them to the task.

Criterion 2: Integration of Movement or Seating Autonomy
  Check: The tutor offers a physical break, a change in seating (e.g., optional chairs), or a brief active task rather than demanding the student sit still.

Criterion 3: Assurance of Psychological Safety
  Check: The tutor explicitly reassures the student that mistakes are acceptable and/or guarantees an environment free from peer ridicule.

Criterion 4: Explicit Social Scripting
  Check: The tutor provides specific, actionable guidance on social manners or expectations (e.g., how to greet someone, how to handle losing) rather than just telling them to join the group.
