### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Think Time
  Check: The tutor explicitly offers the student time to think before requiring a response (e.g., 'Take a moment to think', 'Hands down for a minute').

Criterion 2: Strategy Limitation
  Check: The tutor narrows the focus by summarizing or highlighting no more than two or three key strategies or points at a time.

Criterion 3: Visual or Rephrased Anchoring
  Check: The tutor provides a visual record (e.g., writing down main points) or clearly rephrases peer contributions to anchor the discussion.

Criterion 4: Pressing for Articulation
  Check: The tutor prompts the student to explain their own thinking and does NOT simply provide the mathematical explanation for them.
