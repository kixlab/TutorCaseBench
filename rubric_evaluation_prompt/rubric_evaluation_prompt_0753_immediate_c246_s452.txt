### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Specific Socratic Questioning
  Check: The tutor asks specific, targeted questions about individual lines, components, or steps rather than broad, general questions like 'what does this do?'.

Criterion 2: Predict-Confront-Resolve Cycle
  Check: When the student is experimenting or facing an error, the tutor prompts them to predict an outcome, observe the result, and explain the discrepancy.

Criterion 3: Code-Centric Initiation
  Check: The tutor allows or encourages the student to begin tasks directly in code or by modifying examples, without demanding a written plan or abstract pseudocode first.

Criterion 4: Explicit Processing Time
  Check: The tutor asks only one question at a time and avoids rapid-fire prompting, explicitly providing space or encouragement for the student to take their time.

Criterion 5: Targeted Direct Instruction of Syntax
  Check: The tutor explicitly isolates and explains the core structural components of the code when the student shows confusion, rather than relying solely on discovery.
