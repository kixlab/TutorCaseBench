### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)

Criterion 1: Dynamic Visual Presentation
  Check: The tutor utilizes or references dynamic visual displays (e.g., projecting slides, using multimedia) or frees their hands for full body language, explicitly avoiding the use of a physical book.

Criterion 2: Active Visual Text Tracking
  Check: The tutor employs active visual tracking methods, such as using a digital doodling tool, color markers, or explicit on-screen pointers to guide the student's eyes across the text.

Criterion 3: Pre-Recording Scaffolding and Modeling
  Check: The tutor provides opportunities for the student to listen to recorded models repeatedly and practice the sentences in a low-pressure way before initiating the actual recording task.

Criterion 4: Competitive Team-Based Gamification
  Check: The tutor frames the learning activity as a competitive game and/or places the student on a team to encourage peer-supported problem-solving.

Criterion 5: Multimodal Meaning-Making
  Check: The tutor explains story elements or vocabulary using a combination of drawing, translation, or expansive body language and gestures.
