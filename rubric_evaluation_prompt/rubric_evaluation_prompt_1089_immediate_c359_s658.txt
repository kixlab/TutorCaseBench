### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)

Criterion 1: Academic Challenge Elevation

  Check: The tutor must offer Jasmine the choice to complete the activity with a peer or explicitly turn the task into a shared social activity.

Criterion 3: Explicit Peer-Interaction Scripting
  Check: The tutor must provide a specific, appropriate phrase or script for Jasmine to use to initiate interaction with her peer (e.g., 'Do you want to work together?').

Criterion 4: Avoidance of Compliance-Only Redirection
  Check: The tutor must NOT rely solely on telling Jasmine to return to her paper, reminding her of the schedule, or simply enforcing the original task rules.
