### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not
- The check is principle-level: diverse approaches can earn the point as long as the underlying principle is present
- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point

A tutor response does not need to satisfy every criterion to be considered acceptable; the rubric documents the full set of principles this student needs, and a single response can only address some of them. Score each criterion independently.


────────────────────────────────
### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Objective-to-Task Connection
  Check: The tutor explicitly connects the current question, activity, or prompt to the overarching academic objective or key vocabulary from the lesson, rather than just stating the objective in isolation.

Criterion 2: Academic Focus Over Mechanics
  Check: The tutor frames instructions or corrections around the academic purpose of the task, avoiding responses that focus exclusively on behavioral compliance or the physical mechanics of the activity (e.g., 'fill in the box').

Criterion 3: Objective-Anchored Prompting
  Check: When the student gives a vague or superficial answer, the tutor redirects them by referencing the lesson's objective or standard rather than repeatedly prompting them to guess a specific word.
