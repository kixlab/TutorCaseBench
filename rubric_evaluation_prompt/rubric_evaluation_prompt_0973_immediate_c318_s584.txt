### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Decision-Making Bypass
  Check: The tutor explicitly directs the student on what specific task, subject, or habitual action to start, rather than asking open-ended questions about what they want to do or how to organize their materials.

Criterion 2: Micro-Chunking
  Check: The tutor breaks the immediate assignment into a highly restricted, bite-sized chunk (e.g., focusing on just the next step or a very small subset of problems) rather than presenting the whole task at once.

Criterion 3: Parallel Error Modeling
  Check: When an error occurs, the tutor works through the problem alongside the student or demonstrates the correction process, rather than simply telling the student to find and fix their mistake independently.

Criterion 4: Output Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor offers to physically scribe (type/write) for the student or drastically simplifies the structural requirements of the task (e.g., reducing an essay to a single sentence or basic transition).

Criterion 5: Emotion Validation without Cheerleading
  Check: The tutor explicitly acknowledges or validates the student's difficulty or negative emotions without attempting to cheer them up, minimize the distress, or force a positive outlook.

Criterion 6: Analogical Bridging
  Check: The tutor uses analogies or explicitly connects new academic material to concepts, habits, or experiences the student already knows.
