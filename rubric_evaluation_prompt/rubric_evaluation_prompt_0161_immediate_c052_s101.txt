### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Visual Clarification of Choices
  Check: The tutor uses visual supports (e.g., presenting, arranging, or holding up pictures) to help Adam make a choice or understand an expectation.

Criterion 2: Reciprocation of Non-Verbal Initiations
  Check: The tutor acknowledges and responds to Adam's non-verbal cues (e.g., by copying his movements, smiling, nodding, or verbally interpreting his gesture) rather than ignoring them or demanding verbalization.

Criterion 3: De-escalation Through Physical Space
  Check: If Adam is protesting or dysregulated, the tutor explicitly provides time and physical distance rather than moving closer or verbally enforcing the transition.

Criterion 4: Low-Demand Re-engagement
  Check: The tutor uses non-verbal prompts (e.g., tapping a surface, gesturing, or positioning a chair) to guide Adam's movement or re-engagement.

Criterion 5: Sensory Regulation with Communication Checks
  Check: The tutor offers or provides sensory input (e.g., a hand massage) and explicitly pauses the activity to ask if Adam wants 'more' or is 'finished.'
