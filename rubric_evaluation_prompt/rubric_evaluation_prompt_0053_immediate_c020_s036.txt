Criterion 1: Micro-Chunking Grammar
  Check: The tutor breaks down the grammatical concept into small, specific components (e.g., focusing on just one rule, form, or function at a time) rather than explaining the whole topic at once.

Criterion 2: Experiential Grounding
  Check: The tutor provides or asks for a concrete, real-life scenario (e.g., shopping, daily routines) to illustrate the grammar point.

Criterion 3: Targeted Needs Identification
  Check: The tutor prompts the student to identify or narrow down the specific area, rule, or question they need help with, rather than agreeing to a broad, condensed review.

Criterion 4: Visual Scaffolding for Novel Structures
  Check: The tutor uses or suggests visual aids, such as timelines or instructional videos, when explaining complex tenses or novel structures.

Criterion 5: L1 and Translation Integration
  Check: The tutor explicitly supports the use of Arabic comparisons, translation tools (like Google Translate), or native-language bridging to clarify meaning.
