Criterion 3: Philosophical Clarification
  Check: The tutor must ask the student to expand on, clarify, or provide an example for their argument or viewpoint.

Criterion 4: Neutral Perspective Juxtaposition
  Check: The tutor must present, invite, or highlight a contrasting viewpoint from a peer without injecting their own personal opinion or content into the debate.

Criterion 5: Avoidance of Authoritative Suppression
  Check: The tutor's response must NOT rely solely on authoritative commands (e.g., 'be quiet', 'calm down') or threats of staff intervention to manage the student's behavior.
