### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Empathetic Reframing of Struggle
  Check: The tutor explicitly validates the student's difficulty, expresses empathy, or reframes their struggle as a valid challenge rather than agreeing with or ignoring the student's self-label of laziness.

Criterion 2: Explicit Organizational Instruction
  Check: The tutor provides concrete, step-by-step guidance on exactly how to organize materials, notes, or tasks, rather than simply telling the student to 'get organized' or 'find your homework.'

Criterion 3: Active Task Translation
  Check: The tutor shifts the student away from passive reading or memorization by introducing an active, hands-on, or interactive method for engaging with the current material.

Criterion 4: Persistent Concept Breakdown
  Check: The tutor breaks down the immediate academic concept into smaller parts and offers to explain it step-by-step until the student demonstrates understanding.
