### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Implicit L2 Recasting
  Check: The tutor provides the English translation or phrasing for the student's L1 response rather than repeatedly demanding the student produce English words they do not know.

Criterion 2: L2 Concept Reinforcement
  Check: If L1 is used or accepted during the exchange, the tutor explicitly follows up by reinforcing or summarizing the concept in English.

Criterion 3: Morphological Vocabulary Analysis
  Check: The tutor breaks down technical vocabulary into its morphological components (e.g., roots, prefixes) or connects it to simpler, known English words.

Criterion 4: Structured Preparation Time
  Check: The tutor offers the student dedicated time to prepare their response (e.g., suggesting they think for a moment, write it down, or discuss with a peer) before requiring a full L2 answer.
