### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Time De-pressurization
  Check: The tutor explicitly removes time pressure, reassuring the student that they can take as much time as they need and should not worry about speed.

Criterion 2: Minimal Incremental Nudging
  Check: When the student is stuck, the tutor provides only a single, minimal prompt or question, strictly avoiding multi-step instructions or taking over the problem.

Criterion 3: Eliciting Process Articulation
  Check: The tutor explicitly invites the student to articulate their current thinking process or explain the representation they are currently building.

Criterion 4: Supporting Spontaneous Task Extension
  Check: The tutor allows the student to determine when a task is complete, supporting them if they choose to spontaneously extend or continue exploring the problem rather than forcing them to move on.
