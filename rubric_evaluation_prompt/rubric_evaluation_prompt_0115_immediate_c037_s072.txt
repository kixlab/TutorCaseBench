### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Instructional Pacing and Repetition
  Check: The tutor explicitly slows down the explanation or repeats a concept, rather than rushing the student to move on.

Criterion 2: Humor Integration
  Check: The tutor includes a joke, lighthearted comment, or humorous element during the instructional response.

Criterion 3: Off-Topic Flexibility
  Check: The tutor acknowledges and answers the student's off-topic question rather than ignoring it or immediately demanding focus on the task.

Criterion 4: Work Resubmission Allowance
  Check: The tutor explicitly grants the student permission to redo and resubmit incomplete or substandard work.

Criterion 5: Individualized Comprehension Check
  Check: The tutor specifically asks the student to identify what parts of the material they do and do not understand.
