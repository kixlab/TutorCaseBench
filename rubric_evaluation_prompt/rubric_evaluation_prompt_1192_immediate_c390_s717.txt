### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not

- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Role and Script Provision
  Check: The tutor provides specific suggestions for what role the student should take and/or exact words they can say to their peer.

Criterion 2: Concrete, Action-Specific Questioning
  Check: The tutor asks highly specific, targeted questions about exact actions taken or words spoken, completely avoiding broad, open-ended recall questions (e.g., 'What is your plan?').

Criterion 3: Active Modeling of Play and Communication
  Check: The tutor actively models or role-plays how to use the materials and how to communicate with the peer.

Criterion 4: Reflective Statements and Redirects
  Check: The tutor uses verbal redirects or reflective statements to bridge awkward silences and sustain the ongoing play.
