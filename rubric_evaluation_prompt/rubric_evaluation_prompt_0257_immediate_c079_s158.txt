### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Open-Ended Inquiry
  Check: The tutor asks at least one open-ended question that requires more than a single word to answer, avoiding simple yes/no or basic recall questions.

Criterion 2: Elaboration and Recasting
  Check: The tutor responds to the student's utterance by restating it as a complete sentence, adding descriptive detail, or gently correcting grammar without explicitly stating the student was wrong.

Criterion 3: Conversational Exchange over Compliance
  Check: The tutor engages the student in a back-and-forth verbal exchange rather than issuing a behavioral command or a 'listen and obey' directive.
