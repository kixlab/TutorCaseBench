### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Physical Distance and Intermittent Check-ins
  Check: The tutor's response must explicitly establish physical distance (e.g., stepping away, giving space) and offer support only intermittently rather than hovering.

Criterion 2: Extended Wait Time
  Check: The tutor must explicitly provide or encourage wait time, allowing the student space to process without immediately supplying the answer or a verbal hint.

Criterion 3: Non-Verbal Prompting
  Check: The tutor must utilize or suggest visual, gestural, or environmental prompts rather than relying solely on verbal instructions or verbal hints.

Criterion 4: Structured Independence Incentives
  Check: The tutor must incorporate a structured reward or contingency system (e.g., 'first-then' phrasing, token system, tick chart) to motivate independent effort.

Criterion 5: Focus on Process and Accessibility
  Check: The tutor must focus the interaction on the student's understanding, skill development, or task accessibility (e.g., breaking it down) rather than merely pushing for task completion.
