### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Self-Interest Framing
  Check: The tutor explains the impact of the student's behavior in terms of how it affects their own ability to get what they want, completely avoiding appeals to empathy or the victim's feelings.

Criterion 2: Contingent Reward Phrasing
  Check: The tutor frames instructions using positive contingencies (e.g., 'As soon as you do X, you can have Y') rather than using threats, punishments, or taking things away.

Criterion 3: Preventative De-escalation
  Check: The tutor offers or prompts a time-limited withdrawal (e.g., 'Quiet Time') from the stimulating environment when the student shows signs of dysregulation or inability to stop.

Criterion 4: Use of Shared Self-Regulation Cues
  Check: The tutor uses specific, established cue phrases (such as 'stop button', 'Ready to Learn', or 'Quiet time') to prompt the student to self-regulate.
