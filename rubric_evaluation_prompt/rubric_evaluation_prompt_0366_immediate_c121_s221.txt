### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Minimal Incremental Nudging
  Check: The tutor's response provides exactly one small, incremental prompt or question to move the student forward, explicitly avoiding multi-step explanations or giving away the process.

Criterion 2: Requirement for Process Justification
  Check: The tutor asks the student to articulate how they arrived at their answer or to explain their working, rather than simply confirming if the answer is right or wrong.

Criterion 3: Withholding Unjustified Praise
  Check: The tutor refrains from praising or validating an answer (even if correct) until the student has demonstrated the reasoning or working behind it.
