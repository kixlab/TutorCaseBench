For each criterion in the rubric:

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Scaffolded Task Entry
  Check: The tutor offers a concrete template, model, or step-by-step breakdown to make the task immediately accessible, rather than just telling the student to work.

Criterion 2: Humorous or Low-Friction Redirection
  Check: The tutor uses humor, a light-hearted warning, or a conversational pivot to redirect the student without escalating tension.

Criterion 3: Explicit Behavioral Validation
  Check: The tutor explicitly praises or validates a specific positive behavior, effort, or moment of focus from the student.

Criterion 4: Emotional Acknowledgment and Relational Accountability
  Check: The tutor explicitly acknowledges the student's current emotional state (e.g., frustration, tiredness) or references a prior personal commitment to encourage engagement.

Criterion 5: Absence of Positional Demands
  Check: The tutor does NOT demand compliance based solely on authority, rules, or physical positioning (e.g., avoiding commands like 'sit back down at your desk' or 'do it because I said so').
