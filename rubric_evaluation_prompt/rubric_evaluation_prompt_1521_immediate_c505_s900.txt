### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS

For each criterion in the rubric:

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Explicit Concrete-to-Abstract Mapping
  Check: The tutor's response must explicitly connect a physical object, visual, or action to a specific part of the abstract mathematical sentence (e.g., explaining exactly which box a number goes into based on the objects).

Criterion 2: Simplified Visual Representation
  Check: When suggesting a drawing or visual aid, the tutor must recommend using simple, low-effort marks (like lines, segments, or slashes) rather than complex illustrations.

Criterion 3: Collaborative Role Assignment
  Check: The tutor must propose a shared task where the student is assigned a specific, distinct physical or visual role (e.g., bundling tens, slashing lines) while the tutor or a peer handles another part.

Criterion 4: Grounding in Familiar Examples
  Check: When prompting the student to explain or compare abstract concepts, the tutor must anchor the question in a specific, previously solved example or a familiar story.
