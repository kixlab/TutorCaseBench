### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Neutral, Brief Redirection
  Check: If addressing a disruption, the tutor's correction is concise and delivered in a neutral, non-critical tone, strictly avoiding sarcasm, raised volume, or prolonged lecturing.

Criterion 2: Explicit Action-Oriented Correction
  Check: When redirecting the student, the tutor explicitly states the specific replacement behavior the student should engage in right now (e.g., 'write the answer on your paper') rather than only telling them what to stop doing.

Criterion 3: Behavior-Specific Praise
  Check: When praising the student, the tutor explicitly names the specific positive behavior or academic action the student just performed, rather than using generic praise like 'good job'.

Criterion 4: Brisk Academic Pacing
  Check: The tutor provides immediate, continuous opportunities for the student to answer academic questions or engage with the material, avoiding long pauses or extended periods of passive listening.
