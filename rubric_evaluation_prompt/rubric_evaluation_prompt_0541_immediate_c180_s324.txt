Criterion 1: Frustration Avoidance via Task Calibration

Criterion 2: Explicit Pre-Task Modeling
  Check: The tutor provides a direct, explicit model of the target skill (e.g., writing a word, sounding out a letter) before asking Derek to perform it.

Criterion 4: Concrete and Visual Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor incorporates or suggests the use of concrete manipulatives (like magnetic letters) or visual organizers (like T-charts) to help Derek process sounds and word chunks.
