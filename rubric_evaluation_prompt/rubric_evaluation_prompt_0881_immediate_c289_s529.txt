### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Nurturing Spontaneous Symbolic Play
  Check: The tutor allows, validates, or joins in the student's spontaneous symbolic play rather than interrupting it to force compliance with the original activity.

Criterion 2: Active Facilitation over Verbal Demands
  Check: The tutor uses active facilitation (e.g., modeling, introducing a new engaging element, or modifying the activity) to break unengagement, rather than merely issuing verbal commands like 'look' or 'keep trying'.

Criterion 3: Avoidance of Forced Physical Pointing
  Check: The tutor avoids physically prompting the student's hands to point and does not force compliance-based pointing to test academic knowledge.

Criterion 4: Positive Reinforcement for Shared Attention
  Check: The tutor provides immediate praise, positive feedback, or a rewarding natural consequence when the student demonstrates any level of shared attention, rather than just demanding they look.
