### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Concrete Task Initiation
  Check: The tutor provides a concrete visual aid (e.g., notation, diagrams) or side-by-side guidance for the very first step to unblock the student.

Criterion 2: Direct Accountability
  Check: The tutor introduces a clear, immediate accountability measure, such as announcing an individual test, performance check, or specific deliverable.

Criterion 3: Kinetic Redirection
  Check: The tutor offers a physical, movement-based alternative (e.g., different instruments, physical movement, tactile tools) to channel the student's hyperactive energy.

Criterion 4: Avoidance of Passive or Punitive Escalation
  Check: The tutor avoids giving long verbal instructions and refrains from purely punitive measures (like confiscating or shutting off equipment) in response to disruption.
