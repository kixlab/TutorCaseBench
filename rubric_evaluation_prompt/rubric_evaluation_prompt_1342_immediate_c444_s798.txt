### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Break access through appropriate communication
  Check: The tutor prompts or reminds Charlie, in a communication mode he can access, that he may appropriately request a brief break or delay, and any break offered is contingent on that appropriate communication rather than on tantrum behavior.

Criterion 2: Chosen reinforcement for honest reading effort
  Check: The tutor offers or reminds Charlie of a meaningful reinforcer he can choose and clearly links earning it to completing or genuinely attempting the reading task.

Criterion 3: Smaller reading steps mixed with harder demands
  Check: The tutor adjusts the immediate reading demand into attainable pieces, such as alternating shorter/easier reading requests with harder ones, without abandoning the reading activity altogether.

Criterion 4: Reading is not removed as the tantrum payoff
  Check: The tutor’s response does not end, remove, or indefinitely postpone the reading demand solely because Charlie tantrums; if de-escalation or a break occurs, it is brief, structured, and followed by a return to an attainable reading step.
