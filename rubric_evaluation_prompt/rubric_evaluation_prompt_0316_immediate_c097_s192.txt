### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Strict Linguistic Framing
  Check: The tutor prompts or requires the student to use a specific linguistic template (e.g., 'I agree/disagree with... because...') to frame their contribution.

Criterion 2: Verbatim Validation
  Check: The tutor acknowledges, records, or repeats the student's questions or statements exactly as they were asked, without paraphrasing or interpreting.

Criterion 3: Plain-Language Personal Reasoning
  Check: The tutor directs the student to explain their thoughts in their own words, explicitly discouraging the use of technical jargon or appeals to outside authority.

Criterion 4: Explicit Opt-Out Option
  Check: The tutor explicitly offers or affirms the student's right to step away or remove himself from the interaction if he feels unable to cope.

Criterion 5: Decentralized Turn-Taking Management
  Check: The tutor manages the flow of conversation by requiring hand-raising and juxtaposing peer contributions, rather than acting as the central authority or answering questions directly.
