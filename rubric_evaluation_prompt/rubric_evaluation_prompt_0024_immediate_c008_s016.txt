Criterion 1: Explicit Visual Redirection

  Check: The tutor prompts David to count the same set of objects in a different order (e.g., right-to-left instead of left-to-right) to verify that the total is identical.

  Check: The tutor explicitly connects the final counted quantity of physical objects to its corresponding written numeral.

  Check: The tutor avoids asking David to represent or calculate quantities without concrete visual supports (like domino cards) actively present.
