For each criterion in the rubric:

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Visual Reinforcement
  Check: The tutor provides or suggests a visual or tangible reward (such as drawing a star on the student's work) when the student follows directions.

Criterion 2: Deliberate Wait Time
  Check: The tutor explicitly incorporates a pause or wait time (e.g., waiting 4-5 seconds) after giving an instruction or when the student becomes distracted.

Criterion 3: Non-Verbal Behavior Management
  Check: The tutor uses non-verbal communication (e.g., sad facial expressions, shaking head, or gentle physical touch) to address negative behavior before or instead of using verbal reprimands.

Criterion 4: Direct Impact Feedback
  Check: The tutor addresses aggressive behavior by establishing one-on-one contact (e.g., mutual eye contact) and explicitly stating that the behavior hurts peers or makes them uncomfortable.
