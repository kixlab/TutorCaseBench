### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Embedded Task Choice
  Check: The tutor offers the student at least two distinct options for how to engage with or complete the current activity (e.g., format, topic, or method).

Criterion 2: Explicit Behavioral Goal Setting
  Check: The tutor prompts the student to select, articulate, or visually track a specific behavioral or personal goal for the session (e.g., respect, effort, remembering a fact).

Criterion 3: Behavior-Specific Praise and Tangible Reinforcement
  Check: The tutor provides praise that explicitly names the positive behavior the student demonstrated and/or offers a tangible or visual reinforcement (e.g., a checkmark, a ticket).

Criterion 4: Addressing Pull-Out Dissatisfaction
  Check: The tutor acknowledges the student's frustration about missing class/homework time and/or explicitly connects the intervention activity to helping them succeed in their regular classroom.
