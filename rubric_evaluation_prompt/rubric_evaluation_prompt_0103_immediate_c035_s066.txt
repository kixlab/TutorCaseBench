### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Auditory and Sensory Management
  Check: The tutor's immediate response mutes, disables, or explicitly directs the student away from auditory triggers (e.g., voice chat, loud background noises) rather than just asking them to focus.

Criterion 2: Direct Visual or Physical Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor provides a concrete, immediate visual or physical cue (such as placing a sticker, pointing, or highlighting) to guide the student's physical interaction with the interface.

Criterion 3: Bypassing Complex Text and Audio
  Check: The tutor explicitly instructs the student to ignore unnecessary text/pop-ups or provides a zero-text workaround, strictly avoiding long verbal or written explanations.

Criterion 4: Environmental Distraction Redirection
  Check: The tutor immediately separates the student from the distracting objects (e.g., moving them to a different virtual area) rather than just verbally prompting them to stop playing.
