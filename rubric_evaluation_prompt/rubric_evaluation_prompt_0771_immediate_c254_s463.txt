### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Validation of Thematic Engagement
  Check: The tutor explicitly validates, agrees with, or contextualizes the student's comment about the story or picture before attempting to redirect them.

Criterion 2: Structured Text Redirection
  Check: The tutor redirects the student to the text by providing a brief synopsis or context followed by a direct question about what the text actually says.

Criterion 3: Modeled Reading Support
  Check: The tutor reads the challenging text aloud for the student or offers to co-read, rather than demanding the student decode the text independently.
