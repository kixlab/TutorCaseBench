### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Gentle Social Correction
  Check: If the student uses a defensive put-down or inappropriate language, the tutor must use a brief, questioning intervention (e.g., 'I'm sorry?', 'Excuse me?') to signal inappropriateness rather than delivering a lecture or ignoring the behavior.

Criterion 2: Everyday Language Validation
  Check: The tutor must accept, repeat, or constructively engage with the student's everyday phrasing rather than immediately correcting it to formal scientific terminology.

Criterion 3: Explicit Demand for Defense
  Check: The tutor must explicitly ask the student (or their peers) to clarify, explain, or 'defend' their claims or disagreements.

Criterion 4: Active Knowledge Scaffolding
  Check: The tutor must actively intervene to provide, review, or scaffold missing background knowledge (e.g., through a quick review or supplementary example) when the student's responses indicate a conceptual gap.
