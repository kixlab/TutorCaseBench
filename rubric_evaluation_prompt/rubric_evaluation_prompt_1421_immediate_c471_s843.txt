### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Short-Term Task Granularity
  Check: The tutor directs the student to a specific, bite-sized task to complete right now, rather than discussing or assigning the broader multi-week plan.

Criterion 2: Tutor-Driven Pacing
  Check: The tutor explicitly sets the schedule or target for the immediate session (e.g., 'We will spend the next 10 minutes on X') rather than asking the student what they want to work on or how they want to pace themselves.

Criterion 3: Immediate Accountability
  Check: The tutor establishes a clear, immediate check-in point (e.g., 'Show me your answer as soon as you finish this first problem') to ensure the student is held accountable during the current session.
