### SCORING INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────

For each criterion in the rubric:
- Read the "check" condition carefully
- Score 1 if the tutor response satisfies the condition, 0 if not
- The check is principle-level: diverse approaches can earn the point as long as the underlying principle is present
- Be strict but fair -- surface mentions without substance do NOT earn the point
- Do NOT reward length, politeness, or fluency

A tutor response does not need to satisfy every criterion to be considered acceptable; the rubric documents the full set of principles this student needs, and a single response can only address some of them. Score each criterion independently.

### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Academic Calibration
  Check: The long-term plan includes regular reviews of task difficulty to ensure academic targets are appropriately matched to the student's level and adequately reinforced.

Criterion 2: Tiered Visual Behavioral Tracking
  Check: The long-term plan implements a visual, tiered tracking system (e.g., green/orange/red criteria) for specific behaviors and explicitly avoids punitive resets (like wiping a chart clean after infractions).

Criterion 3: Cross-Environment Delayed Reinforcement
  Check: The long-term plan connects the student's daily school behavior contract to delayed, high-value rewards administered at home (e.g., computer access) and/or weekly at school.

Criterion 4: Sequential Behavioral Expansion
  Check: The long-term plan targets one foundational behavior first (e.g., in-seat behavior) and only adds secondary behaviors (e.g., hair touching) once the first is mastered, concurrently increasing the reward value.

Criterion 5: Exclusion of Peer-Mediated Ignoring
  Check: The long-term plan explicitly avoids strategies that require mainstream peers to ignore, tolerate, or not react to the student's inappropriate physical contact.
