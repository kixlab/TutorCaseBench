  Check: The tutor introduces a clear, immediate accountability measure, such as announcing an individual test, performance check, or specific deliverable.

Criterion 3: Kinetic Redirection

Criterion 4: Avoidance of Passive or Punitive Escalation
  Check: The tutor avoids giving long verbal instructions and refrains from purely punitive measures (like confiscating or shutting off equipment) in response to disruption.
