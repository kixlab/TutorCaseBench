### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Simultaneous Conceptual-Procedural Linking
  Check: The tutor explicitly pairs a physical manipulative action or drawing with the writing or explaining of a mathematical sentence in the same step.

Criterion 2: Simplified Visual Representation
  Check: The tutor prompts the use of simple, abstract visual aids (like lines or tallies) to represent numbers, rather than detailed or complex illustrations.

Criterion 3: Explicit Bundling Guidance
  Check: The tutor provides explicit, step-by-step guidance on how to physically group or bundle items into tens and where to place the corresponding digits.

Criterion 4: Conceptual Grounding of Procedures
  Check: The tutor ensures every procedural step (like setting up a vertical form or carrying a number) is explained through a conceptual model, avoiding rote copying.
