### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)

Criterion 1: Positive Reinforcement for Vulnerability

  Check: The tutor delivers feedback conversationally and explicitly invites the student to ask questions or clarify the comments in the moment.

Criterion 3: Incremental Goal Scaffolding
  Check: When the student offers an initial goal or reflection, the tutor prompts her to identify 'one more thing' to improve or expand upon.

Criterion 4: De-escalation of 1-on-1 Pressure
  Check: If the student shows hesitation, nervousness, or uses single-word answers, the tutor does not force continued 1-on-1 interrogation, instead pivoting to a lower-pressure approach or validating her discomfort.
