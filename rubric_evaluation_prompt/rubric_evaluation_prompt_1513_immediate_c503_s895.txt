### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Simultaneous Concrete-Abstract Linking
  Check: The tutor explicitly connects a mathematical symbol, operation, or procedural step to a concrete visual or physical representation (e.g., manipulatives, simple drawings) rather than just providing abstract rules.

Criterion 2: Step-by-Step Validation
  Check: The tutor directly answers Esther's request for validation on her current step or provides clear, 1:1 guidance on the immediate next action, rather than telling her to work independently or 'try her best'.

Criterion 3: Simplified Visual Representation
  Check: When utilizing visual aids or drawings, the tutor directs the student to use simple marks (e.g., lines, slashes, boxes) rather than detailed illustrations.

Criterion 4: Contextual Grounding
  Check: The tutor frames the mathematical problem within a familiar, real-world context or story rather than presenting it as a purely abstract equation.
