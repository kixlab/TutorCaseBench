Criterion 1: Daily Task Granularity

Criterion 2: Technological Sensory and Modality Accommodation
  Check: The tutor offers or permits the use of technology (e.g., a computer for typing, headphones/MP3 for noise, or visual projections) to help the student engage with the task.

Criterion 3: Empathetic Inquiry into Avoidance
  Check: The tutor asks open, caring questions about the student's feelings or reasons for not completing work (e.g., asking 'why' they are quiet or didn't do homework), rather than simply reprimanding them.

Criterion 4: Supported Environmental Shifts
  Check: If the tutor suggests moving to a quiet environment, they explicitly pair this move with active, individualized instructional support rather than sending the student away alone.
