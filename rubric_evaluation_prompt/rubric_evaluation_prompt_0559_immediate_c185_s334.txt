### RUBRIC (variable-length checklist)
────────────────────────────────

Criterion 1: Structured De-escalation Space
  Check: The tutor directs the student to a specific cool-down space or reflection period (e.g., a thinking corner or chair for a set time) before attempting to discuss or resolve the conflict.

Criterion 2: Empathy-Building Inquiry
  Check: The tutor asks a direct perspective-taking question (e.g., 'How would you feel if someone did that to you?') to prompt empathy for the affected peer.

Criterion 3: Facilitation of Voluntary Resolution
  Check: The tutor asks an open-ended question prompting the student to decide the next step (e.g., 'What do you need to do now?') and strictly avoids explicitly commanding the student to apologize.
